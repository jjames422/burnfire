## Priority purchases

- **VIP Shop:** Stamina, Omni Shards, Epigraph Chests, then speedups.
- **Alliance Shop:** build and research speedups, Survivor Coins, Omni Shards, Raven Essence Chests, then Epigraphs.
- **Honor Shop:** Epigraph Chests first; buy Omni Shards and Raven Essence until the Raven reaches Level 110, then shift toward gear.
- **Expedition Shop:** Omni Shards, Epigraph items, chests, Raven Essence, Gear Stones, Recruit Tickets, resource bags, then speedups.
- **Sanctuary Shop:** Raven Essence and UR Omni Shards first, followed by Antidotes, SSR shards, Gear Stones, tickets, and lower-priority skill materials.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Protect your Omni Shards">Universal shards are most valuable when a hero’s dedicated shards are scarce. Avoid spending them on heroes whose shards are reliably available elsewhere.</Callout>
