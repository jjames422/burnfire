## What Alliance Duel is

Alliance Duel is a weekly alliance-versus-alliance competition. To take part, an alliance needs 20+ members on Sunday and has to be in the top 32 on the server by Might. Six phases run Monday through Saturday, each one settles daily at server time 00:00, and the alliance with the higher total wins.

It's one of the easiest events to waste resources on. The biggest advantage isn't having the strongest accounts — it's timing.

## Victory points

| Phase | Focus | Victory points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Enhance Raven | 1 |
| 2 | Territory Development | 2 |
| 3 | Tech Research | 2 |
| 4 | Hero Growth | 2 |
| 5 | War Preparation | 2 |
| 6 | Raid the Enemy | 4 |

The side with more victory points at the end of the week wins, which is why Phase 6 matters more than any other day — it's worth double the earlier phases.

## Phase by phase

**Phase 1, Enhance Raven.** Falcon Quests are the best scorer here (15,000 once), with Raven Essence at 2,875. Stamina, Antitoxin, Raven Fruit, Grain and Timber all score too. This phase also carries points buffs: +15% to all points excluding diamond purchases, and +35% on Falcon Quest points.

**Phase 2, Territory Development.** Construction speedups and Building Might score, but the big hitters are UR Covert Operations (86,250) and UR Caravans (115,000). Recruiting a survivor scores 1,725 once.

**Phase 3, Tech Research.** Raven Gear Chests score enormous points — a Lv5 is worth 103,500, a Lv6 310,500, and a Lv7 931,500. Research speedups, Tech Might, Study Scrolls and Falcon Quests also count.

**Phase 4, Hero Growth.** Recruit heroes and spend your hero growth items on this day. Hero shards are worth 11,500 for UR, 4,025 for SSR and 1,150 for SR. Skill badges and Antitoxin contribute as well.

**Phase 5, War Preparation.** Train troops today — higher tiers score more, with a T10 soldier worth 143 points. Training boosts, building and tech growth, speedups and Falcon Quests also score. If you run the [Waterfall Doctrine](/guides/waterfall-doctrine), this is the day you promote.

**Phase 6, Raid the Enemy.** Defeating soldiers in your specific duel matchup gives the most points (a T10 defeated in the matchup is worth 74.3). General defeats and even your own troop losses give points too, just less. Healing speedups, UR Covert Operations and UR Caravans score here as well.

## How BF2 plays it

Save resources for the phase that pays for them instead of spending randomly through the week. Use Falcon Quests on days 1, 3 and 5. Save UR Covert Ops and UR Caravans for days 2 and 6. Coordinate for raid day, because Phase 6 is worth 4 victory points on its own. And watch alliance chat and mail before you spend anything big.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Two rules that cost people points">Reward chests for stages 4–9 require Alliance Duel tech research. And if you change alliances during Alliance Duel, your points don't count toward the alliance total for that day.</Callout>
