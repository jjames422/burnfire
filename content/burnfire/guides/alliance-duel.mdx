Alliance Duel can be one of the easiest events to waste resources on if you’re not paying attention to which phase is active. The biggest advantage isn’t simply having the strongest accounts — it’s timing.

## How it works

Alliance Duel is a weekly alliance-vs-alliance competition. Only alliances with 20+ members on Sunday and in the top 32 by server Might can participate. Six phases run Monday to Saturday, daily settlement is at server time 00:00, and the side with more victory points at the end of the week wins.

| Phase | Focus | Victory points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Enhance Raven | 1 |
| 2 | Territory Development | 2 |
| 3 | Tech Research | 2 |
| 4 | Hero Growth | 2 |
| 5 | War Preparation | 2 |
| 6 | Raid the Enemy | 4 |

## Phase by phase

- **Phase 1 — Enhance Raven:** a Falcon Quest scores 15,000 and Raven Essence 2,875. Stamina, Antitoxin, Raven Fruit, Grain, and Timber also score. Buffs: All Points +15% (excluding diamond purchases) and Falcon Quest Points +35%.
- **Phase 2 — Territory Development:** construction speedups and Building Might score. Big scorers are UR Covert Operation (86,250) and UR Caravan (115,000); recruiting a survivor scores 1,725.
- **Phase 3 — Tech Research:** Raven Gear Chests score huge points — Lv5 = 103,500, Lv6 = 310,500, Lv7 = 931,500. Research speedups, Tech Might, Study Scrolls, and Falcon Quests also score.
- **Phase 4 — Hero Growth:** recruit heroes and spend hero growth items. Hero Shards: UR = 11,500, SSR = 4,025, SR = 1,150. Skill badges and Antitoxin also contribute.
- **Phase 5 — War Preparation:** train troops; higher tiers score more (a T10 soldier trained = 143 points). Training boosts, building/tech growth, speedups, and Falcon Quests also score.
- **Phase 6 — Raid the Enemy:** defeating soldiers in the specific duel matchup gives the most points (a matched T10 defeat = 74.3 points). General defeats and even troop losses give fewer points. Healing speedups, UR Covert Operation, and UR Caravan also score.

## BF2 strategy

- Save resources for the correct phase instead of spending them randomly.
- Use Falcon Quests on Days 1, 3, and 5.
- Use UR Covert Ops and UR Caravans on Days 2 and 6.
- Save hero shards for Hero Growth, troop training for War Preparation, and research resources for Tech Research.
- Phase 6 is worth 4 victory points — coordinate for raid day.
- Watch alliance chat and mail before spending big resources.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Key rules">Reward chests for stages 4–9 require Alliance Duel tech research. If you change alliances during Alliance Duel, your points don’t count toward the alliance total for that day.</Callout>
