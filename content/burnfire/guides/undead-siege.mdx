Undead Siege is an alliance defense event. The **Death Prison** sends **20 waves of Blights** against our members’ Sanctuaries, and every member defends their own city. The more waves we survive together, the better the alliance rewards — so **participation matters just as much as strength**.

## What it is

- An **alliance defense event** against **20 waves of Blights**.
- **R4/R5 starts it** after the alliance has gathered enough **Falcon Quest clues** (see clue farming below).
- Once started, **the Death Prison appears and attacks member Sanctuaries.**

## Before it starts

You get **about 180 seconds of prep time** after the event is triggered. Use it:

- **Put your strongest squad in Garrison / Walls.** This is what defends your city.
- **Send or request reinforcements before the timer ends.**
- **Remember: shields do NOT stop Undead Siege attacks.** Your wall defenders are your only protection — if you pulled them off the wall for safety, put them back.
- **Members usually need 48+ hours in the alliance** to receive rewards. New members can still help, but may not be paid out.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Restore your wall">If you normally leave your wall empty to limit losses from raids, restore defenders before Undead Siege. See [The Empty Wall Paradox](/guides/empty-wall-paradox).</Callout>

## How the battle works

- **The 20 waves attack automatically.** You don’t need to send marches at the Blights — your garrison fights them.
- **If your Sanctuary loses a wave, your city stops being attacked for the rest of the run.** You’re out, and you stop adding waves to the alliance total.
- **Strong members should reinforce weaker members** so they survive more waves.
- **A run goes on a 48-hour cooldown after completion.**

## Rewards and scoring

There are two reward tracks:

**Personal rewards** are based on the waves *you* survived. Checkpoints are at waves **1, 4, 8, 10, 13, 16, and 20**.

**Alliance rewards** are based on the **total waves survived by everyone** added together. The **Stage 10 max alliance goal is 1,200 total waves**.

The **main prize is huge amounts of Antitoxin**, which is what you level heroes with.

**Why participation matters:** the alliance score is a sum of everyone’s waves. Twenty members each surviving 15 waves (300 total) beats ten members surviving all 20 (200 total). Keeping more members alive for longer is how the alliance total grows.

## BF2 best strategy

- **Pick the highest difficulty MOST active members can survive.**
- **Do NOT choose difficulty based only on the strongest players.** If the difficulty knocks out half the alliance early, the total waves collapse.
- **Pair stronger players with weaker members** for reinforcements, so weaker members last longer.
- **Participation matters** — more members alive means better alliance rewards.

## Fast clue farming

The event can’t start until enough Falcon Quest clues are collected. Not all sources are equal:

| Source | Clues |
| --- | --- |
| Rebels | 300 each |
| Blight Legion | 300 each |
| Wandering Blight | 300 each |
| Many routine tasks | 10 each |

One Rebel or Blight kill is worth 30 routine tasks. Focus clue farming on those three targets.

## Quick checklist

- Farm clues from Rebels, Blight Legion, and Wandering Blight.
- When the event triggers, use the ~180 seconds: strongest squad on the wall, reinforcements sent.
- Don’t count on a shield — it won’t help.
- Stronger members: reinforce your assigned partner.
- Stay alive as long as you can — every wave counts toward the alliance total.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Verify before starting">Stage requirements can vary by Sanctuary level and Might. Always verify the difficulty screen in-game before starting.</Callout>

<Callout variant="lore" title="Win as a team">The goal is not just a few strong players surviving — the goal is keeping as many BF2 members alive as possible.</Callout>
