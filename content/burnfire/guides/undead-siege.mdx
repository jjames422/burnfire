## What it is

Undead Siege is an alliance defense event. Once enough Falcon Quest clues have been gathered, an R4 or R5 starts it, the Death Prison appears, and it sends 20 waves of Blights against members' Sanctuaries.

## Before it starts

You get roughly 180 seconds of prep, which goes faster than you'd think. Put your strongest squad in Garrison on your Walls, and send or request reinforcements before the timer runs out. Two things catch people out every time: **shields do not stop Undead Siege attacks**, and you usually need to have been in the alliance for 48+ hours to qualify for rewards.

## How the battle plays out

All 20 waves attack automatically. If your Sanctuary loses a wave, your city stops being attacked for the rest of the run — so you're out, and the alliance loses every wave you would have survived after that. That's why strong members reinforcing weaker ones matters so much here. After a run finishes it goes on a 48-hour cooldown.

## Rewards and scoring

Personal rewards depend on how many waves *you* survived, with checkpoints at waves 1, 4, 8, 10, 13, 16 and 20. Alliance rewards are based on the total waves survived by everyone combined, and the stage 10 maximum goal is 1,200 total waves. The main prize is a huge amount of Antitoxin, which is exactly what you want for hero levels.

## How BF2 plays it

We pick the highest difficulty that *most* of our active members can survive — not a level chosen around the strongest few players. Stronger players get paired with weaker members for reinforcements, because the goal isn't a handful of people surviving, it's keeping as many of us alive as possible. More members standing at the end means better alliance rewards for everyone.

## Farming clues fast

The best clue sources are Rebels, Blight Legion and Wandering Blight, which are worth 300 clues each. A lot of routine tasks only give 10, so if we're trying to get the event open, those three are where your stamina should go.
