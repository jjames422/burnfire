Undead Siege is an alliance defense event where the Death Prison sends 20 waves of Blights against our members’ Sanctuaries. The more waves we survive together, the better the alliance rewards — so participation matters just as much as strength.

## What it is

- An alliance defense event against 20 waves of Blights.
- R4/R5 starts it after enough Falcon Quest clues are gathered.
- The Death Prison appears and attacks member Sanctuaries.

## Before it starts

- Put your strongest squad in Garrison / Walls.
- Send or request reinforcements before the timer ends — you get about 180 seconds of prep time.
- Members usually need 48+ hours in the alliance for rewards.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Shields don’t help here">Shields do NOT stop Undead Siege attacks. Make sure your wall defenders are restored — see [The Empty Wall Paradox](/guides/empty-wall-paradox).</Callout>

## How the battle works

- The 20 waves attack automatically.
- If your Sanctuary loses, your city stops being attacked for the rest of the run.
- Strong members should reinforce weaker members.
- A run goes on a 48-hour cooldown after completion.

## Rewards and scoring

- Personal rewards are based on your own waves survived, with checkpoints at waves 1, 4, 8, 10, 13, 16, and 20.
- Alliance rewards are based on total waves survived by everyone. The Stage 10 max alliance goal is 1,200 total waves.
- The main prize is huge amounts of Antitoxin.

## BF2 strategy

- Pick the highest difficulty **most** active members can survive.
- Do NOT choose difficulty based only on the strongest players.
- Pair stronger players with weaker members for reinforcements.
- Participation matters — more members alive means better alliance rewards.

## Fast clue farming

The best clue sources are Rebels, Blight Legion, and Wandering Blight, worth 300 clues each. Many routine tasks give only 10 clues.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Verify before starting">Stage requirements can vary by Sanctuary level and Might. Always verify the difficulty screen in-game before starting.</Callout>
