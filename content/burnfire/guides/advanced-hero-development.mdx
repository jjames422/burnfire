Building powerful heroes requires more than increasing Might. Advanced development means understanding formations, faction advantages, skill progression, equipment, and resource efficiency.

## Squad architecture

Build around five heroes with complementary roles:

- **Frontline:** two durable tanks to absorb damage.
- **Backline:** two damage dealers to eliminate threats.
- **Support:** one hero providing useful buffs, healing, or other abilities.

Example formation: **Louis + Ulfrid** in front, with **Red Lady + Marlena + Harper** behind.

<Callout variant="warning" title="A sample, not a guarantee">This is a sample formation, not a guaranteed optimal team. Review hero skills and faction bonuses before committing resources.</Callout>

## Faction advantages

Faction matchups and formation bonuses can significantly influence combat effectiveness. Warrior counters Warlock, Ranger counters Warrior, and Warlock counters Ranger; countering factions take 20% less damage from the faction they counter. Lineup bonuses range from +5% ATK/HP/DEF for three matching heroes up to +20% for five.

Compare your lineup’s active bonuses against the benefits of countering enemy factions. Never sacrifice essential abilities solely to increase a formation bonus.

## Advanced hero progression

Invest resources based on your squad’s needs:

- **Levels:** use Antitoxin to increase hero levels.
- **Stars:** use hero shards to advance star progression.
- **Skills:** prioritize abilities that strengthen each hero’s role.
- **Equipment:** improve weapons, armor, gloves, and boots according to their attributes.
- **Sanctuary:** upgrade your Sanctuary when hero progression reaches its level restriction.

Avoid distributing rare resources equally across heroes you rarely use. Higher Might does not automatically mean better performance in every role.

## Battle testing

1. Record your current formation’s results.
2. Change one hero or upgrade at a time.
3. Fight comparable opponents.
4. Compare damage, survival, casualties, and completion time.
5. Keep adjustments that improve actual performance.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Final principle">Build for synergy, invest strategically, and validate improvements through combat. The strongest squad is not necessarily the one with the highest Might.</Callout>
