Building strong heroes takes more than pushing Might up. Advanced development means understanding formations, faction advantages, skill progression, equipment, and how to spend resources efficiently.

## Squad architecture

Build around five heroes whose roles complement each other: two durable tanks on the frontline to absorb damage, two damage dealers in the back to eliminate threats, and one support hero bringing buffs, healing or other useful abilities.

As an example, you might run **Louis and Ulfrid** up front with **Red Lady, Marlena and Harper** behind them. That's a sample formation, not a guaranteed optimal team — review hero skills and faction bonuses before you commit resources to any lineup.

## Faction advantages

Faction matchups and formation bonuses can swing a fight noticeably. Compare the bonuses your lineup has active against what you'd gain by countering the enemy's factions instead. But never give up an essential ability purely to pad a formation bonus.

<Figure src="/images/guides/advanced-hero-development-2.png" alt="Advanced Hero Development field guide, page 2" caption="Page 2 of 3." width={1086} height={1448} />

## Progression, by resource

Spend based on what your squad actually needs. Antitoxin raises hero levels, and hero shards push star progression. For skills, prioritize the abilities that strengthen each hero's role. Upgrade weapons, armor, gloves and boots according to their attributes. And when hero progression hits its level restriction, that's your cue to upgrade the Sanctuary.

The common mistake is spreading rare resources evenly across heroes you barely use. Concentrate them. Higher Might doesn't automatically mean better performance in every role.

<Figure src="/images/guides/advanced-hero-development-3.png" alt="Advanced Hero Development field guide, page 3" caption="Page 3 of 3." width={1086} height={1448} />

## Battle testing

Record how your current formation performs. Then change one hero or one upgrade at a time and fight comparable opponents. Compare damage, survival, casualties and completion time, and keep only the adjustments that improve real results.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The final principle">Build for synergy, invest strategically, and validate improvements through combat. The strongest squad isn't necessarily the one with the highest Might.</Callout>
