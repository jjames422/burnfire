Shards are the tightest resource in your first two weeks. This list only covers heroes you can
realistically pull or unlock early — no point ranking a hero you won't see for a month.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Shard priority">
  Commit shards to **one** damage hero and **one** support hero early. Spreading thin across
  three or four half-built heroes is the single biggest early-game time loss.
</Callout>

## Tier list

| Hero | Role | Tier | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marrow | Damage | S | Scales cleanly, cheap to shard-up, strong against early plague-zone mobs |
| Sister Agatha | Support | S | Team-wide thirst reduction, keeps early rally squads alive longer |
| Coil | Damage | A | Good ceiling but needs gear investment before it pays off |
| Bramwell | Tank | A | Best early wall-hold hero, weak damage so pair with Marrow or Coil |
| Ratcatcher | Scout | B | Fine for PVE clearing, skip for PVP |

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't shard Ratcatcher first">
  New players consistently over-invest in Ratcatcher because it's the easiest early pull.
  It caps out fast — treat it as a placeholder, not a long-term investment.
</Callout>

## Free-to-play path

If you're not spending, prioritize Sister Agatha over Marrow. A support hero staying alive
through your first raid window matters more than raw damage before you have gear to back it up.
