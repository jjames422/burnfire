The Halloween Ball includes five activities. Use this checklist to plan your progress and avoid missing rewards.

## The five activities

1. **Trick-or-Treat (Event Pass):** open the event and review target quests. Complete objectives to upgrade your pass, track milestones, and claim available rewards. Prioritize quests that overlap with normal gameplay.
2. **Masquerade Ball (Dice & Tiles):** spend Halloween Coins to roll dice across the board. Item and Event tiles offer different outcomes; higher-level tiles give better rewards. Blessing Chests may appear randomly. Review the board and your coin balance before rolling — never assume a chest is guaranteed.
3. **Gown Fitting (Server Cooperation):** collect masks through the methods shown in-game and work with Doctors across the server to raise the shared Gown Level. Alliance leaders can share confirmed collection methods and progress updates.
4. **Halloween Minigame (Leaderboard):** review each stage objective, clear stages, and check the leaderboard. Confirm scoring and any attempt limits in-game before planning a ranking strategy.
5. **Halloween Bazaar (Reward Exchange):** exchange Magic Hats earned at the ball for rewards. Review prices and limits first, choose priority items, then spend. Check the countdown before the event ends.

## Daily routine

- **Log in:** check quests, progress, and timers.
- **Play:** complete quests, roll dice, collect masks, attempt stages.
- **Log out:** claim rewards, check Gown progress, review the Bazaar and remaining currency.
