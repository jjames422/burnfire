## Event overview

The Halloween Ball is a limited seasonal event starting **October 26**. It bundles **five separate activities**, each with its own goal and its own currency. The easiest way to miss rewards is to treat it as one event — so this guide walks through each activity, what it asks of you, and how they fit together.

| Activity | What you do | Currency / item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trick-or-Treat | Complete target quests to upgrade your pass | Quest progress |
| Masquerade Ball | Roll dice across a board of tiles | Halloween Coins |
| Gown Fitting | Collect masks to raise a server-wide Gown Level | Masks |
| Halloween Minigame | Clear stages and climb a leaderboard | Stage clears |
| Halloween Bazaar | Exchange hats for rewards | Magic Hats |

## 1. Trick-or-Treat: Pass Quests

An event pass with target quests. Completing objectives upgrades your pass and unlocks milestone rewards.

- Open the event and **read each objective first**.
- **Collect milestones as you progress** — don’t let claimable rewards pile up.
- **Prioritize quests that overlap with normal gameplay.** If you’re already gathering, training, or building, make sure those actions count toward a quest.

## 2. Masquerade Ball: Dice & Tiles

A board game. Spend **Halloween Coins** to roll dice and move across the board.

- The board has **Item tiles** and **Event tiles**, which give different outcomes.
- **Higher-level tiles offer better rewards.**
- **Blessing Chests can appear randomly** — watch for them, but they aren’t guaranteed.
- **Review the board and your coin balance before rolling.** Don’t assume a chest will appear.

## 3. Gown Fitting: Server Cooperation

A cooperative goal shared by the whole server. **Collect masks** through the methods shown in-game and contribute them alongside Doctors across the server to raise the shared **Gown Level**.

- Server cooperation helps everyone — every mask you contribute moves the shared level.
- Alliance leaders can share confirmed collection methods and progress updates so everyone knows where to find masks.

## 4. Halloween Minigame: Leaderboard

A stage-based minigame with a ranking.

- **Review each stage objective** before attempting it.
- **Clear stages** and check your placement on the leaderboard.
- **Aim for steady progress and consistent clears** rather than burning attempts.
- Confirm scoring and any attempt limits in-game before planning a ranking push.

## 5. Halloween Bazaar: Reward Exchange

The shop at the end. Use **Magic Hats** earned during the event to exchange for your preferred rewards.

- **Review the Bazaar before spending anything** — check prices and purchase limits.
- Choose your priority items first, then spend hats accordingly.
- **Check the countdown** so you don’t end the event with unspent hats.

## Recommended daily routine

1. **Check quests and event timers.**
2. **Complete daily tasks** — quests, dice rolls, mask collection, minigame stages.
3. **Track coins, masks, and hats** so you know what each activity has left.
4. **Claim rewards and review progress**, including the server Gown Level.
5. **Use remaining event currency before the event ends.**

## What’s confirmed

- The Halloween Ball **starts October 26**.
- **Five event activities** are announced.
- **Blessing Chests can appear randomly** on the Masquerade Ball board.
- **Magic Hats are exchanged in the Bazaar.**

Featured heroes in the event art include Annie (UR), Claire (SSR), Marlena (UR), Arthur (UR), Shadow (UR), Cynthia (UR), Grenwald (SSR), and Daskal (UR).
