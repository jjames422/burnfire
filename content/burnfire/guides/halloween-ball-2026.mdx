The Halloween Ball starts October 26 and is made up of five activities. They feed into each other, so it helps to know the whole loop before you start spending anything.

## Trick-Or-Treat: the event pass

Open the event and look over the target quests first. Completing objectives upgrades your pass, and the milestones along it are where the rewards come from. Prioritize the quests that overlap with what you're already doing in normal play — those are free progress.

## Masquerade Ball: dice and tiles

You spend Halloween Coins to roll dice across a board. Item tiles and Event tiles give different outcomes, and higher-level tiles give better rewards. Blessing Chests can pop up at random. Look at the board and your coin balance before rolling, and never assume a chest is guaranteed.

## Gown Fitting: the whole server pitches in

Collect masks through the methods shown in-game and work with Doctors across the server to raise the shared Gown Level. This one is cooperative, so once we've confirmed the best collection methods, leadership will share them along with progress updates so everyone can chip in.

## Halloween Minigame: the leaderboard

Read each stage's objective, clear the stages, and keep an eye on the new leaderboard. Before planning any ranking push, confirm in-game how scoring works and whether there's an attempt limit.

## Halloween Bazaar: cashing in

The Magic Hats you earn at the ball get exchanged here for rewards. Check prices and limits first, decide which items are your priority, and spend your hats in that order. Watch the countdown so you don't sit on hats after it closes.

## A simple daily routine

When you log in, check your quests, progress and timers. While you're playing, work through quests, roll your dice, collect masks and attempt stages. Before you log out, claim your rewards, check Gown progress, and look over the Bazaar and whatever currency you have left.
