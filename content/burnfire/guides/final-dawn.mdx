## Build Might with purpose

Final Dawn is a one-time seven-day beginner event. Complete milestone tasks to earn Recruit Tickets, speedups, resources, stamina, Antitoxin supplies, and the final UR Sword reward.

The strongest early investments are the **Sanctuary, Research Lab, Hospital, troop training, and hero gear**. Keep construction and research active, train continuously, and use alliance help to shorten timers.

## Early squads

The Warrior-focused lineup uses Arthur and Bella up front with Marlena, Celia, and Claire. If difficult content demands more sustain, the alternative keeps Arthur and Marlena with Bella, Claire, and Stellar.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Do not postpone the week">Log in daily, complete all tasks, claim milestones as they unlock, and plan ahead. Waiting until day seven creates unnecessary pressure and wasted progress.</Callout>
