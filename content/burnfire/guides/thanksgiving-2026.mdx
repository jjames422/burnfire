The Thanksgiving Event is live. When I first wrote this guide, all we had was the preview. Since then, the official team has posted a full breakdown with in-game screenshots, so this version covers every part of the event by its real name. The screenshots below are theirs.

It runs for about a week. The timers in the official screenshots read just under 7 days at launch, which puts the end around November 29, but check the countdown in your own game. Like the Halloween Ball, it's a bundle of separate activities with their own currencies, and the easiest way to lose rewards is to treat it as one event.

## The event at a glance

| Activity | What you do | What it runs on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cranberry Dash | Event pass: daily quests earn cranberries that unlock reward tiers | Pass points (cranberries) |
| Surprise Harvest | Slot-machine draw: match symbols for bigger rewards | Harvest Coins |
| Thanksgiving Market | Exchange shop, including the Harvest Chef skin | Festival items from the other activities |
| Thanksgiving Feast | Submit Fruit Baskets to raise a server-wide feast level | Fruit Baskets |
| Harvest Day | Shows where Fruit Baskets come from, with a daily cap | Normal gameplay |
| Joy Multiplied | New block-merge minigame with stages and a clear ranking | Daily free attempts |
| Heartwarming Blend | Top-up milestones with the Acorn Lodge curio | Spending |

## The prize: Harvest Chef and Culinary Inspiration

The headline reward is the **Harvest Chef** territory skin, and it's **permanent**. According to its preview screen, it gives:

- **When equipped:** Hero ATK +5%
- **Just for owning it:** Hero ATK +5% and Hero DEF +5%

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-harvest-chef.png" alt="Harvest Chef territory skin preview showing Hero ATK +5% when equipped and Hero ATK +5% and Hero DEF +5% when owned" caption="Harvest Chef: the bonuses for equipping and owning it." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={527} height={633} narrow />

There's also a permanent territory effect, **Culinary Inspiration**, which increases all your heroes' skill damage by 1.5%.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-culinary-inspiration.png" alt="Culinary Inspiration item description: a permanent territory effect that increases all heroes skill damage by 1.5%" caption="Culinary Inspiration: +1.5% skill damage for all heroes." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={502} height={270} narrow />

You redeem the skin in the **Thanksgiving Market** with festival items you collect from the event's activities. Because the "owned" bonus stays active even when you're showing a different skin, this one is worth working toward if you can.

## 1. Cranberry Dash (the pass)

Cranberry Dash is the event pass. You earn cranberries from its **Daily Quests**, and every cranberry milestone unlocks a row in **Basic Rewards**. There are three reward columns. The first is the free track, and the other two look like the paid tiers (the Surprise Harvest screen has a Battle Pass button). A **Pass Chest** unlocks once you reach 9,100 pass points.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-cranberry-dash.png" alt="Cranberry Dash pass screen with three reward columns, cranberry milestones and a Pass Chest that unlocks at 9,100 pass points" caption="Cranberry Dash: three reward columns and the Pass Chest at 9,100." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={431} height={723} narrow />

Do the daily quests every day, because a skipped day is progress you don't get back. Claim your rows as you go. If you're going to buy a paid tier, buy it early so you get its rewards for the whole run.

## 2. Surprise Harvest (the slot draw)

This is the Harvest Coin draw, and it's a three-reel slot machine. Each pull costs **1 Harvest Coin**, and matching identical symbols pays more. There's a **5X** switch to pull five at a time, and a **Skip** box in the corner to skip the spin animation if it's slowing you down.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-surprise-harvest.png" alt="Surprise Harvest slot machine with the Draw button, 5X switch, Skip box, Daily Timed Quest and draw counter" caption="Surprise Harvest: 1 coin per pull, with the 5X switch and Skip box at the bottom." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={420} height={723} narrow />

Two more things on this screen are easy to miss:

- **Daily Timed Quest:** a small daily goal on top of the reels with its own reward. Finish it every day.
- **Draw counter:** in the top-left corner, it counts toward a reward (0/30 at the start).

The **Exchange** button on this screen takes you to the Market, and **Preview** shows what the draw can pay. Look at the preview before you spend your coins.

## 3. Thanksgiving Market (the exchange)

The Market is where the festival items you've collected turn into rewards, including the Harvest Chef skin. Before you buy anything, read the **Exchange Reminder** the preview told us about. That's where this shop's rules are.

Decide early whether you're going for the skin. If you are, count what it costs, save for it, and don't spend on filler first. Spend whatever's left on speedups or resources before the event ends.

## 4. Thanksgiving Feast (server-wide)

This one is a group effort. You **submit Fruit Baskets** to the feast, and every 10 you submit earns 1 EXP for the feast level, plus rewards for you. The level bar looks shared by the whole server (the bar read 1,738/8,000 in the official screenshot while that account held only 51 baskets), much like Gown Fitting at Halloween, so every basket helps everyone. A **Rank** button shows the top contributors.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-feast.png" alt="Thanksgiving Feast screen with the feast level bar, Submit button and Rank button" caption="Thanksgiving Feast: every 10 Fruit Baskets is 1 EXP." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={426} height={723} narrow />

## 5. Harvest Day (where Fruit Baskets come from)

Harvest Day isn't really a separate activity. It's the screen that shows where Fruit Baskets drop and how many you've collected today. The sources shown include:

- **Falcon Tower**
- **Resource Monsters**
- **Elite Blights**
- **Black Ops**

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-harvest-day.png" alt="Harvest Day screen listing Fruit Basket sources: Falcon Tower, Resource Monster, Elite Blight and Black Ops, with a daily max of 300" caption="Harvest Day: where Fruit Baskets come from, capped at 300 a day." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={435} height={723} narrow />

There's a **daily cap of 300 Fruit Baskets**, so try to reach it every day instead of trying to catch up at the end. Each source has a **Go** button that takes you straight to it.

## 6. Joy Multiplied (the new minigame)

This is the new block-merge game from the preview. Blocks pile up layer by layer, matching colors merge on their own, and **10 blocks of the same color stacked in one cell clear**. It's split into **stages**, and each one has its own clear reward (the first two give speedups and resources). There's a **Clear Rankings** board and a **Special Pack** for extra attempts.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-joy-multiplied.png" alt="Joy Multiplied minigame screen with 10 free attempts, Clear Rankings, Special Pack and stage clear rewards" caption="Joy Multiplied: free attempts, stage rewards and the clear ranking." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={443} height={722} narrow />

You start with **10 free attempts** (the screen shows Free 10/10, and the + button buys more). Check whether they refresh daily on your server, and don't let free ones go unused. A few tips that help:

- **Give each color a home.** Build each cell toward one color so it reaches 10 instead of filling up with mixed blocks.
- **Don't bury a color.** A block underneath a different color can't help its own stack until the top clears.
- **Look before you place.** Merges happen on their own, so check which cell a block will join before you drop it.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Reported bug">On opening night, one player reported getting soft-locked after finishing a round, tapping back and making one more move. The game froze and wouldn't restart until they rebooted their phone. Until it's fixed, let the round finish cleanly before you leave the screen.</Callout>

## 7. Heartwarming Blend (top-up rewards)

This is the spending part of the event. Total top-ups during the festival unlock milestone rewards, from 1,000 up to 100,000. The headline reward is the **Acorn Lodge** curio, which shows Hero HP +108,800 at max level. It's completely optional. Nothing else in the event needs it.

<Figure src="/images/guides/thanksgiving-heartwarming-blend.png" alt="Heartwarming Blend top-up milestones from 1,000 to 100,000 with the Acorn Lodge curio showing Hero HP +108,800 at max level" caption="Heartwarming Blend: top-up milestones and the Acorn Lodge curio." credit="Official Last Asylum team" width={428} height={722} narrow />

The official team is also offering a **Thanksgiving Harvest Pact** on their official top-up website, and they recommend buying through the official site if you do spend.

## Daily routine

1. Do the Cranberry Dash daily quests and claim any rows you've reached.
2. Farm Fruit Baskets up to the 300 cap (Falcon Tower, Resource Monsters, Elite Blights, Black Ops), then submit them to the Feast.
3. Finish the Surprise Harvest daily timed quest and spend your Harvest Coins.
4. Use your free Joy Multiplied attempts.
5. Check the official Discord for gift codes.

## How BF2 plays it

Fruit Baskets are the backbone of this event, so everyone farms to the 300 cap every day and submits to the Feast. The server level only goes up if we all do our part. Go for the Harvest Chef skin if you can get there, because its owned bonus of +5% ATK and DEF is permanent. Do the pass and the slot's daily quest before anything else, play your free minigame attempts every day, and spend everything in the Market before the timer runs out.
