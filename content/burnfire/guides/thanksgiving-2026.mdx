The Thanksgiving Event opens on **November 23**. Like the Halloween Ball, it's a bundle of separate activities, and each one runs on its own currency. Most of the rewards people miss come from mixing those up, or from leaving a currency unspent when the timer runs out.

Everything below comes from the official Thanksgiving preview posted in the Last Asylum Discord. The preview doesn't give costs, reward tables or an end date, so I've marked which parts are confirmed and which you should check in game on day one. I'll update this page once the event is live.

## The four parts at a glance

| Activity | What you do | What it runs on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thanksgiving Pass | Finish event quests and daily objectives to level up the Pass | Quest progress |
| Harvest draw | Draw for rewards. The better your patterns match, the better the prize | Harvest Coins |
| Voucher exchange | Trade vouchers for the items you want | Thanksgiving Vouchers |
| Block-merge minigame | Stack blocks until 10 of one color clear | Minigame attempts |

## 1. The Thanksgiving Pass

You level up the Pass by completing event quests and daily objectives, and it pays out as you go. That's the whole mechanic, and it's also the easiest part of the event to fall behind on.

Do the daily objectives every day, even the boring ones, because a missed day is progress you can't get back. Before you start your normal routine, read the quest list. If you're already going to gather, train or build, make sure you're doing it in a way that counts toward a quest. And claim tiers as you reach them instead of letting them stack up until the last day.

The preview doesn't mention a paid tier. The Full Moon Night pass in September had one, though: a $5 upgrade that added a second reward track and doubled the Pass XP from eligible tasks. If the Thanksgiving Pass works the same way and you're going to buy it, buy it early, while the double XP still has time to pay off.

## 2. The Harvest Coin draw

You spend **Harvest Coins** on a draw, and the preview says the closer your patterns match, the richer the reward. It doesn't say what the patterns are or what each match pays, so open the draw's info page before your first pull and look at the prize table.

The last draw event, Full Moon Night, also had a milestone track that paid out at set draw counts (starting at 30 draws), on top of each draw's own reward. If this draw has one too, figure out how many coins you'll earn over the whole event and pick a milestone you can actually reach. Running out of coins two draws short of a milestone is the worst outcome.

## 3. Thanksgiving Vouchers and the exchange

**Thanksgiving Vouchers** are spent in the exchange on the items you choose. The preview specifically says to look at the **[Exchange Reminder]** before you go, so read it first. It's where the rules for this shop live, including anything about limits or what happens to leftover vouchers.

Look at the whole shop before you buy anything. Pick the one or two things you actually want, check how many vouchers they cost and how many times you can buy them, and save for those. Spend what's left on speedups or resources near the end. Don't spend vouchers on filler on day one and then come up short for the item you wanted on day six.

## 4. The new block-merge minigame

This one is new. The preview introduces it under "Joy Multiplied": blocks pile up layer by layer, blocks of the same color merge on their own, and **once 10 blocks of the same color stack in one cell, they clear.**

Since clears only happen at 10 of one color in a single cell, the main thing is to keep your cells sorted:

- **Give each color a home.** Try to build each cell toward one color so it reaches 10 instead of filling up with mixed blocks.
- **Don't bury a color.** A block sitting under a different color can't help its own stack until the top is cleared.
- **Look before you place.** The merges happen on their own, so check which cell a block will join before you drop it.

The preview doesn't say how many attempts you get or whether there's a leaderboard. The Full Moon Night minigame gave 10 free attempts a day and sold extra attempts and tools, so check the limits on day one and use your free attempts every day.

## Gift codes

The preview says the team has lined up gift codes and more in the community during the event, so check the official Discord often. Holiday codes in this game tend to be short-lived, so redeem a code the day you see it.

There's also a community prompt for opening day. On November 23, the official Discord asks in #general who in the Asylum you most want to thank this harvest season.

## Daily routine

1. Do the Pass's daily objectives and claim any tiers you've reached.
2. Use your free minigame attempts.
3. Check how many Harvest Coins you have against the draw milestone you're aiming for.
4. Count your vouchers against the exchange item you're saving for.
5. Check the official Discord for new gift codes.

## What's confirmed, and what to check in game

**Confirmed by the official preview:** the event opens November 23; the four activities above; Harvest Coins go into the draw, and better pattern matches pay more; Thanksgiving Vouchers go into the exchange, with an Exchange Reminder to read first; the minigame clears 10 blocks of the same color stacked in one cell; and gift codes will be shared in the community during the event.

**Check on day one:** when the event ends, what the Pass costs and what it pays, how you earn Harvest Coins and vouchers, the draw's prize and milestone tables, exchange prices and limits, and the minigame's daily attempts.

## How BF2 plays it

Do the Pass objectives every day before anything else, because they're free progress that disappears if you skip a day. Read the draw and exchange info screens before spending a single coin or voucher, then pick a target in each: a draw milestone you can reach, and an exchange item worth saving for. Play your free minigame attempts every day and keep your colors sorted. On the last day, spend whatever you have left so nothing goes to waste.
