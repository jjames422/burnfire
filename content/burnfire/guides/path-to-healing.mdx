## Two conditions, one reward path

Path to Healing requires both VIP 5 during the event window and completion of the daily task groups across days one through five. The advertised rewards include 15 UR Arthur fragments, 30 Celia fragments, and a full completion chest.

Daily categories move from foundational logins and upgrades through Arena, troop training, research, recruiting, and final combat tasks. Check the event panel every day because all three categories must register before reset.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Prioritize long timers early">Begin Research, troop training, Raven Tower levels, and Combat Power growth before the final day. Stockpile speedups and resources, but claim every reward before the event closes.</Callout>
