Season 2 is the Frozen Era, and it changes more than the scenery. The whole map resets into a frozen Snow Realm, your territory now has a temperature you have to manage, and the server splits into two factions fighting over Fire Crystals. Everything below comes from the in-game Frozen Era preview, so it's what the game itself says is coming.

## Temperature is the new survival stat

Your territory has a temperature, and it changes your stats. Keep it high and you get bonus stats. Let it swing too far either way and you're in trouble.

If your temperature drops to **−20°C**, a Freeze countdown starts. Don't get it back above −20°C in time and your territory freezes. While frozen you lose Advanced Relocate and Rally, which is the last thing you want in the middle of a fight. Some units on the map can freeze too, not just player territories.

Run too hot and the opposite happens. At **40°C** a Burn countdown starts, and if you don't cool down in time your territory catches fire and starts losing Durability. You can put it out yourself or call alliance members for help, but if it stays above 40°C the countdown starts over.

There are two ways to deal with a frozen neighbor:

- **Icebreaking.** When a target is frozen, you spend Stamina to send a squad to break the ice before you can attack or occupy it.
- **Heating.** You can send a Scout Squad to an ally's territory and spend Charcoal to raise their temperature.

On top of that, blizzards roll through the Snow Realm on a regular basis and drag regional temperatures down. The stronger the blizzard, the bigger the drop. This is an alliance-wide problem, not a solo one. Watch each other's temperature.

## Era resources

The Frozen Era brings three resources of its own:

- **Fire Crystal** is the important one. It's rare, it gives your territory steady heat, and when the era ends, Alliance and faction rankings are decided by how many Fire Crystals you hold. You get them by occupying cities and strongholds, taking Blazing Furnaces from rival alliances, and capturing key structures during Faction Clash.
- **Charcoal** builds the Iron Mine Workshop and boosts your Iron output.
- **Iron Ore** builds the Blazing Furnace, which raises your Temperature and RES.

Hunting monsters in the Snow Realm drops large amounts of era resources, so keep hunting. Any Charcoal or Iron Ore you haven't used when the era ends gets converted into Lord's Specialty EXP. It isn't wasted, but you get far more out of it by spending it during the era.

## Furnaces and era buildings

You can build several Snow Realm buildings during the Frozen Era:

- **Blazing Furnace:** raises territory Temperature and RES. This is your main building for the season.
- **Iron Mine Workshop:** boosts Iron output.
- **Faction Pillar:** gives a bonus to Faction Heroes.
- **Alliance Furnace:** an alliance building that warms the zones around it once it's activated. You can overload it for extra heat, but that costs more Charcoal.

**RES** matters as much as temperature. The plague has turned the Snow Realm's creatures into abominations, and upgrading your Blazing Furnace raises your RES so you can stand up to them. The game warns that attacking with low RES can mean heavy losses.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Nothing carries over">Every era building, and the Alliance Furnace, is removed when the era ends. Build for the season you're in.</Callout>

## The Snow Realm map

When the Frozen Era starts, alliances relocate to the Snow Realm as a group. Every city an alliance held before is cleared, and Alliance Homebases get moved to the outer zones of the map. Everyone starts from the edges again.

From there:

- **Strongholds** are scattered across the Snow Realm and held by mutated behemoths. Clear them out to retake the strongholds and get Charcoal production going again.
- **Cities** can heat the zones around them, but monster raids have broken that heating. Recapture a city to restore its warmth across its radius.
- **The Royal City** is sealed off by ice and can't be reached directly. You have to occupy a **Lv.6 City** first before you can launch an assault on it. Keep your alliance territories connected as you push toward it.

The King matters more than usual this season. The King can't be changed from the start of the Frozen Era until the first Royal City Scramble opens, and the King chooses the kingdom's faction. Every kingdom should think hard about who holds the Royal City in the final Scramble before the era begins.

## Order or Nirvana

The harsh conditions have split the kingdoms into two factions:

- **Order Alliance** wants absolute order. It's fighting to restore the old rule of the Royal City and bring order to the chaos.
- **Nirvana Alliance** rejects coercive control. It wants to burn away the remnants of the old era through the plague and build a new world.

**Faction Clash** is the big faction-against-faction event. From Monday through Saturday (server time), both factions compete for points through regular gameplay, and the faction with the higher score becomes the attacker. The battle itself happens at the Royal City of the defending faction's leading kingdom, and Lords from all eight servers can join. Inside the Royal City and its Turrets, Lords of the same faction can't attack each other.

The battle can last up to four hours, and it ends early if Royal City occupation reaches 100%. Both sides earn Faction Fire Crystals based on how they did at the Turrets and the Royal City. Those only count toward faction totals, not alliance or kingdom Fire Crystal counts.

## The Fire Crystal War

Separately from Faction Clash, alliances fight each other directly over Fire Crystals on a fixed weekly rhythm (server time):

- **Tuesday and Friday: declare war.** One side attacks and the other defends, and you can only declare on enemy alliances with comparable Fire Crystal rankings. The attacking side rotates each week.
- **Defenders can call for help.** Once the declaration phase ends, if several alliances have declared on the same target, the defender can invite other same-faction alliances with similar rankings to help defend.
- **Wednesday and Saturday: battle.** If the attackers win, they plunder part of the Fire Crystals stored in the defending alliance's Furnace.

Plan your week around those four days. Have people online on Wednesday and Saturday, because a Furnace with nobody defending it is a Fire Crystal donation.

## Hero power-ups

The Frozen Era is also when a lot of heroes get stronger:

- **Awakening:** Arthur, Shadow and Joker unlock Awakening progressively through the era.
- **Exclusive Weapons:** Red Lady, Billy and Zoya unlock theirs progressively through the era.
- **Promotion:** Ash unlocks the Promotion feature.
- **New in the Tavern:** Zoya, Louis and Ulfrid join the Tavern, so you'll have a chance to recruit them.

Some heroes also get era bonuses against monsters. Zoya, Red Lady, Joker and Ash deal more damage to monsters, while Arthur, Shadow and Billy take less damage from them. If you own the **Phantom** city skin, it grants extra RES during the era.

## Other things to watch for

- **Era Quests:** new quests with their own rewards. They disappear when the era ends, so finish them on time.
- **Achievements:** new personal and alliance achievements with large rewards.
- **Falcon Tower:** new Frozen Era quests that pay out era resources.
- **Visitors:** new visitors appear on the map. Interact with them or fight them for era resources.
- **Alliance Gathering Point:** beat an era Rally Monster and you may uncover a gathering point that up to 10 alliance members can harvest.
- **Alliance Milestones:** new era milestones the whole alliance works toward together.

## How BF2 plays it

Temperature first, always. A frozen territory can't Rally or use Advanced Relocate, so nobody goes offline cold, and anyone who sees an ally dropping toward −20°C sends a Scout Squad with Charcoal. Spend your era resources during the season instead of letting them convert at the end. Get the Blazing Furnace up early for the RES, and don't attack plague monsters you aren't ready for. And keep Wednesdays and Saturdays clear for the Fire Crystal War, because that's where the season's rankings are won and lost.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short version">Stay between −20°C and 40°C, look after your allies' temperature, build your Blazing Furnace early, and be online for war days. Fire Crystals decide the season.</Callout>
