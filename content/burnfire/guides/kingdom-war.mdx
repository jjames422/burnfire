Kingdom War is the big one — it decides your server’s standing against another server each week, and it’s easy to get lost in the first time you’re pulled into one.

## What Kingdom War is

Every week, your entire server is matched against another server of similar age, and the two kingdoms compete head-to-head. It runs through the Alliance Duel system — the event your alliance completes on your own server feeds points into your kingdom’s war standing.

## How points are earned

Alliance Duel runs in phases (enhancing your Raven, alliance management, tech research, troop training, and more) completed on your own server. You don’t need to win every phase: hitting the points threshold is an automatic win for your alliance, so unused resources can be saved for the next opponent instead of wasted. Winning a phase and becoming that phase’s MVP earns bonus points toward Kingdom War itself. See the [Alliance Duel guide](/guides/alliance-duel) for the phase-by-phase breakdown.

## The weekly rhythm

Every day of the week carries a “plunder the opposing server” objective alongside that day’s domestic focus.

- **Friday:** plunder your assigned opponent’s server, verify your 24h/12h shields are active, train heroes to fill your barracks, and push Raven quests and Might-boosting speedups.
- **Saturday is the peak:** shields matter more than ever, and this is when the direct cross-server raid phase (Raid the Enemy) hits hardest. Expect real incoming attacks, not just simulated ones.

## Before you jump in

- Join an active alliance — most of this is alliance-based, and solo players get left out of the rewards entirely.
- Buying resource packs and using speedups both add points in every phase, so don’t hoard them past the point they’re useful.
- Most alliances push hardest right before the server refreshes — plan your own last-second contribution too.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Not participating this week?">Keep a shield active on your base at all times (the free 8-hour shield covers most of the danger window), don’t leave troops out in the open or on the alliance flag, and keep relocation items stocked in case your position gets found. Kingdom War doesn’t care whether you’re playing along — if you’re unshielded, you’re a target either way.</Callout>
