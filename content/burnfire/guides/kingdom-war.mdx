## What Kingdom War actually is

Every week your entire server is paired against another server of a similar age, and the two kingdoms go head-to-head. It's the event that decides where our server stands, and it's easy to get lost the first time you're pulled into one because most of it doesn't look like a war at all. It runs through Alliance Duel, the event your alliance is already completing on your own server, and those results feed straight into your kingdom's war standing.

## How the points get counted

Alliance Duel runs in phases — enhancing your Raven, alliance management, tech research, troop training and so on — and all of it happens on home turf. You don't need to win every phase. Hitting a phase's points threshold counts as an automatic win for the alliance, so once we're over the line, anything left over can be saved for next week's opponent instead of thrown away. Winning a phase outright and taking that phase's MVP spot earns bonus points toward Kingdom War itself.

## The weekly rhythm

Every day of the week carries a "plunder the opposing server" objective on top of that day's own domestic focus, but two days carry far more weight than the rest.

On **Friday**, plunder your assigned opponent's server, make sure your 24h or 12h shield is actually active, train heroes to fill your barracks, and push your Raven quests and Might-boosting speedups.

**Saturday is the peak.** Shields matter more than any other day, because this is when Raid the Enemy — the direct cross-server raid phase — hits hardest. Expect real incoming attacks, not simulated ones.

## Before you jump in

Be in an active alliance. Almost all of Kingdom War is alliance-based, and solo players end up left out of the rewards entirely. Resource packs and speedups add points in every phase, so don't hoard them past the point they're useful. And most alliances push hardest right before the server refreshes, so plan your own last-second contribution too.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Sitting this week out?">Keep a shield on your base the whole time — the free 8-hour shield covers most of the danger window. Don't leave troops out in the open or parked on the alliance flag, and keep relocation items stocked in case your position gets found. Kingdom War doesn't care whether you're playing along; if you're unshielded, you're a target either way.</Callout>
