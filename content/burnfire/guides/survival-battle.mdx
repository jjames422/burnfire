Survival Battle runs every day, and it's one of the easiest events to get value from without spending anything extra. You're already building, training, researching and leveling heroes. Survival Battle just pays you for doing each of those things in the right four-hour window instead of whenever you happen to log in.

## How it works

Each day you're dropped into a group of 12 to 20 players at a similar Sanctuary level, so you're competing against people your own size, not the whales on the server.

The day is split into **six rounds of four hours**, starting at 00:00 server time. Every round has a theme — Build Territory, Train Soldiers, Technology Research, Enhance Raven or Enhance Heroes — and only actions that match the current theme score. Speedups you burn in a Build round do nothing for you in a Train round.

## What scores in each theme

| Theme | What earns points |
| --- | --- |
| Build Territory | 10 per 1 minute of construction speedup used, 1 per Building Might gained |
| Train Soldiers | 10 per 1 minute of training speedup used, 28 per Lv.9 soldier trained (higher tiers score more) |
| Technology Research | 10 per 1 minute of research speedup used, 1 per Tech Might gained |
| Enhance Raven | 100 per Stamina spent, 1 per 10 Raven Fruit used |
| Enhance Heroes | 400 per hero recruit, 1 per 1,950 Antitoxin used |

Buying packs also scores in every theme, so if you were going to buy one anyway, buy it during a round.

The standouts are hero recruits at 400 points each and Stamina at 100 each. Saving your recruit tickets and a full Stamina bar for the right round is the single easiest way to clear stage rewards.

## Stage rewards and Survival Medals

Every round has **four stage rewards** that unlock as your score climbs, and each round's rewards are separate, so you can claim all four again in the next theme. Claiming stage rewards also gives you **Survival Medals**, and medals unlock the three daily chests:

| Survival Medals | Daily chest |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Blue Chest |
| 8 | Purple Chest |
| 18 | Orange Chest |

You won't reach 18 medals by playing one round. The Orange Chest comes from picking up stage rewards across several themes through the day, which is why knowing the schedule matters.

## The weekly schedule

All times are server time. The themes rotate in a fixed order, so the same slot lands on a different theme each day.

| Server time | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00 | Heroes | Build | Train | Tech | Raven | Heroes | Build |
| 04:00 | Build | Train | Tech | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train |
| 08:00 | Train | Tech | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train | Tech |
| 12:00 | Tech | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train | Tech | Raven |
| 16:00 | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train | Tech | Raven | Heroes |
| 20:00 | Heroes | Build | Train | Tech | Raven | Heroes | Build |

<Callout variant="warning" title="Check your own event screen">This schedule has matched across several servers, but rotations can differ. Open Survival Battle in game and confirm the next round's theme before you spend anything big.</Callout>

## Double-dipping with Alliance Duel

Survival Battle and [Alliance Duel](/guides/alliance-duel) score many of the same actions, so some rounds pay you twice for the same item. These are the slots to save for:

| Day | Alliance Duel phase | Survival Battle rounds that line up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Enhance Raven | 00:00 Heroes, 16:00 Raven, 20:00 Heroes |
| Tuesday | Territory Development | 00:00 Build, 20:00 Build |
| Wednesday | Tech Research | 04:00 Tech |
| Thursday | Hero Growth | 08:00 Heroes |
| Friday | War Preparation | 08:00 Build, 12:00 Train, 16:00 Tech |

Monday's hero rounds count because Antitoxin also scores in Alliance Duel's Raven phase. Friday is the big one: War Preparation scores training, building and tech growth, so three Survival Battle rounds line up with it. If you run the [Waterfall Doctrine](/guides/waterfall-doctrine), the Friday 12:00 Train round is where your promotions should land.

## How BF2 plays it

Don't chase every round. Pick the rounds where you actually have something to spend, and hit them hard enough to clear the stage rewards.

Save your recruit tickets for an Enhance Heroes round, ideally Thursday 08:00 when Alliance Duel's Hero Growth day pays for them too. Let your Stamina fill up and spend it in an Enhance Raven round. Hold your speedups for the matching theme instead of tapping them the moment a timer annoys you. And before you log off, check what the next round is — if it's one you can score in, finish your queues just after it starts, not just before.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short version">Same items, same effort, better timing. Match your spending to the round, aim for 18 medals a day, and save your biggest items for the slots that also count in Alliance Duel.</Callout>
