## What the event rewards

Crystal Cluster Valley places allies and enemies on the same event map, but ranking is personal. Event-only troop losses do not affect the army at home, so deploy aggressively up to your barracks limit.

## Recommended timeline

- **0–2 minutes:** land and use the free teleport if positioning is poor.
- **2–10 minutes:** defeat Plague Rats for skill progress and gather only from Level 3 nodes.
- **10–12 and 20–22 minutes:** prioritize the Instant Crystal Waves.
- **23–30 minutes:** contest the Sacred Cave; the final seven minutes decide the meaningful rank.

Run multiple marches, bring stamina Diamonds, and keep teleport items ready. The single free return refreshes every six minutes; further repositioning costs more.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Timing matters more than raw march count">Missing a wave or activating the wrong skill at the wrong moment can cost more than fielding fewer troops.</Callout>
