## How Hunt Battle works

Hunt Battle is an alliance-wide event, not alliance versus alliance. Blights spawn in waves on our own territory and everyone fights them together. It needs 20 or more active members to run, and only an R4 or R5 can start it — so if you're keen to go, ping an officer rather than waiting for it to appear.

## The waves

Once it's started there's a 5-minute prep window with no combat, which is your time to get squads home and healed. After that come 15 waves, each harder than the last. Every march has a fixed 15-second travel time no matter where you are, so distance isn't an excuse and nobody gets a head start.

## The difficulty call

This is the decision that matters most. Difficulty locks in the moment it's chosen, and you get one attempt per event. A full clear on a lower difficulty beats a partial clear on a harder one, so we pick the level the alliance can actually finish, not the one that looks impressive.

## Wave orders

| Waves | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Deploy nonstop. Keep every free march moving. |
| 6–10 | Focus fire on the targets that get called in chat. |
| 11–15 | Have your buffs live *before* wave 11 starts. |
