Hunt Battle is an alliance event where waves of Blights attack our territory and the whole alliance fights them off together. It has one attempt per event and a difficulty you can’t change once chosen, so the plan matters before anyone sends a march. Read this before our first run so nobody’s guessing when it goes live.

## 1. How Hunt Battle works

- **Alliance-wide event — not alliance vs alliance.** There’s no enemy alliance; everyone fights the Blights.
- **Requires 20+ active members.** “Active” matters — members who don’t show up don’t help clear waves.
- **Only R4 or R5 can start it.** Leadership picks the time and difficulty.
- **Blights spawn in waves on our territory.**

## 2. The waves

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Prep window (5 minutes) | No combat yet. Get online, get squads ready, and position. |
| Waves 1–15 | 15 waves arrive, **each harder than the last**. |

**Every march has a fixed 15-second travel time, for everyone.** Distance doesn’t matter — a member far from the action reaches a target as fast as one next to it. That means everyone can contribute to every wave, and nobody can be “too far away.”

## 3. The difficulty call

- **Difficulty locks in once chosen.**
- **One attempt per event.**
- **A full clear beats a harder partial clear.**

**Why:** a harder difficulty looks tempting, but if the alliance stalls on wave 11, the run ends there. A lower difficulty the alliance actually finishes pays out the full clear. Leadership picks the difficulty based on who will realistically be online and fighting — not on what our top accounts could solo.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Clear beats ambition">Pick the difficulty the alliance can actually finish. You only get one attempt.</Callout>

## 4. Wave orders

The waves get harder as they go, so the plan changes in three stages:

- **Waves 1–5: deploy nonstop.** Early waves are the easiest. Keep marches going constantly so they’re cleared fast and nothing piles up.
- **Waves 6–10: focus fire on called targets.** Waves get tougher. Follow the targets called in chat instead of spreading out — concentrated damage clears a target before it overwhelms anyone.
- **Waves 11–15: buffs live before wave 11.** The final waves are the hardest. Have your buffs activated **before** wave 11 starts, not after it’s already hitting.

## 5. BF command checklist

Each role has one job:

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **God of War** | Runs the event. |
| **Recruiter** | Confirms headcount — makes sure we have 20+ active members before starting. |
| **Goddess** | Checks prep before combat. |

## Before the run: member checklist

- Be online when the event is scheduled — we need 20+ active.
- Use the 5-minute prep window to get your squads ready.
- Have buffs ready to activate before wave 11.
- Watch chat for called targets from wave 6 onward.
