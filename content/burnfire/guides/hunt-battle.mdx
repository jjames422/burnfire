## How Hunt Battle works

- It’s an alliance-wide event — not alliance vs alliance.
- It requires 20+ active members.
- Only R4 or R5 can start it.
- Blights spawn in waves on our territory.

## The waves

A 5-minute prep window comes first, with no combat yet. Then 15 waves arrive, each harder than the last. Every march has a fixed 15-second travel time for everyone.

## The difficulty call

Difficulty locks in once chosen, and there’s one attempt per event.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Clear beats ambition">A full clear beats a harder partial clear. Pick the difficulty the alliance can actually finish.</Callout>

## Wave orders

- **Waves 1–5:** deploy nonstop.
- **Waves 6–10:** focus fire on called targets.
- **Waves 11–15:** have buffs live before wave 11.

## BF command checklist

- God of War runs the event.
- Recruiter confirms headcount.
- Goddess checks prep before combat.
