## The objective

Cursed Potions are the only Event Points, and they're also what unlocks the Event Chests. Each Contest lasts 30 minutes and its rankings only count for that round. Your best placement across all the rounds is what decides your final rewards, so one bad round doesn't sink you.

## When it happens

The Preview runs Tuesday to Friday and reveals the first earthquake and which Warzones are contested. After that, Contest rounds alternate with Preparation windows that reveal the next location, and results come after the final round. It doesn't open during [Kingdom War](/guides/kingdom-war).

## Where to play

**The Outskirts** work like normal — shields behave as usual, so this is where you gather for lower-risk progress.

**The Cursed Land** is the high-reward zone. You can relocate in from outside without using a relocation item, and your base earns 40 Potions per second while it's there. The catch is that other Doctors can breach you.

## What each resource returns

| Resource | While gathering | On completion | Once returned to your territory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing Kit | 40/sec | +300 | — |
| Ancient Beast Bone | 80/sec | +1,500 | 30/sec each |
| Runic Idol | 160/sec | +6,000 | 120/sec each |

## Stack your income, then protect it

Your core base (40/sec) plus one returned Idol (120/sec) already gives you 160 Potions per second of passive income before you've gathered anything else. That's why Idols are worth fighting for. If you get breached, though, you can lose stored Bones and Idols and the future income that comes with them. Potions you've already earned stay yours.

## The 30-minute plan

Start gathering right away. If you're moving into the core, set your defenses first. Fighting tends to pick up around minute 10, so have relocation options ready by then. Stronger players can scout and gather for roughly the first 15 minutes, then go after suitable high-Potion targets. Leadership will coordinate so we're not throwing troops into fights we don't need.

## Rewards

Chests give Fame Medals, Raven Essence, Diamonds and Herbs. Personal ranking pays Alliance Coins and Study Scrolls. Alliance ranking, which every member gets, includes a UR Curio Chest, UR Hero Omni Shards and Fame Medals.
