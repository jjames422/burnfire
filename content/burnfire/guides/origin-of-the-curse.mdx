## The objective

Cursed Potions are the only Event Points and unlock Event Chests. Each Contest lasts 30 minutes, and rankings count only that round. Your highest placement across rounds determines final rewards.

## When it happens

The Preview (Tuesday–Friday) reveals the first earthquake and the contested Warzones. Contest rounds alternate with Preparation windows that reveal the next location, and results follow the final round. The event does not open during Kingdom War.

## Where to play

- **Outskirts:** shields work normally. Gather here for lower-risk progress.
- **Cursed Land:** relocate in from outside without consuming a relocation item. Your base earns 40 Potions/sec, but other Doctors can breach it.

## Exact resource returns

| Resource | Gathering | On completion | Once returned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing Kit | 40/sec | +300 | — |
| Ancient Beast Bone | 80/sec | +1,500 | 30/sec in your territory |
| Runic Idol | 160/sec | +6,000 | 120/sec in your territory |

## Stack and protect income

Core base (40/sec) + one returned Idol (120/sec) = **160 Potions/sec passive**, before gathering. A breach may remove stored Bones and Idols and reduce future income, but Potions already earned remain yours.

## The 30-minute plan

Gather from the start. Set defenses if entering the core. Combat may intensify near minute 10, so have relocation options ready. Strong players can scout and gather for about 15 minutes, then attack suitable high-Potion targets. Alliance leaders should coordinate to limit unnecessary fighting.

## Rewards

- **Chests:** Fame Medals, Raven Essence, Diamonds, Herbs.
- **Personal ranking:** Alliance Coins, Study Scrolls.
- **Alliance ranking (all members):** Curio Chest (UR), UR Hero Omni Shards, Fame Medals.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Switching alliances">After switching alliances, only points earned after joining count toward the new alliance.</Callout>
