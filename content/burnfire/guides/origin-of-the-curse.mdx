## The objective

An earthquake has torn open the **Cursed Land**, releasing immense corruption. Your goal is to gather as many **Cursed Potions** as possible. Cursed Potions are the **only source of Event Points**, and Event Points unlock **Event Chests**.

Each **Contest round lasts 30 minutes**, and each round’s ranking counts **only the Potions earned in that round**. Your **highest placement across all rounds** determines your final rewards — so a weak round won’t hurt you.

## When it happens: the four phases

| Phase | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Preview | Tuesday–Friday | Reveals the first earthquake location and which Warzones are contested. |
| 2. Contest | 30 minutes per round | Gather, defend, and plunder to collect Cursed Potions. Each round’s ranking counts only that round’s points. |
| 3. Preparation | Between Contest rounds | Reveals the next earthquake location and the Warzones competing for it. |
| 4. Results | After all Contest rounds end | Settles final personal and alliance ranking rewards. |

Contest and Preparation alternate until the last round. **The event does not open during Kingdom War.**

## Where to play: Outskirts vs Cursed Land

The map has a safe outer ring and a dangerous core.

| | Outskirts (safe zone) | Cursed Land (core) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shields | Work normally | Your base can be attacked by other Doctors |
| Getting there | You’re already there | Relocate in from outside — **no relocation item needed** |
| Base income | — | **40 Potions/sec** just for having your base there |
| Best for | Steady, lower-risk gathering | Highest yields and passive income |

Resource points increase in level toward the center — **the closer to the core, the higher the yield and reward.**

## Resource returns

Three resource types can be gathered. The two advanced ones also give **territory yield** — once you bring them back to your territory, they keep generating Potions every second.

| Resource | Tier | While gathering | On completion | Territory yield once returned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing Kit | Basic | 40/sec | +300 | — |
| Ancient Beast Bone | Advanced | 80/sec | +1,500 | +30/sec per returned Bone |
| Runic Idol | Advanced | 160/sec | +6,000 | +120/sec per returned Idol |

**Why Idols matter most:** a Runic Idol gathers four times as fast as a Healing Kit, pays 20 times the completion bonus, and then keeps paying 120/sec for the rest of the round once it’s home.

## Stacking passive income

Passive income adds up. Example:

- Base in the Cursed Land: **40/sec**
- One returned Runic Idol: **+120/sec**
- **Total: 160 Cursed Potions/sec** — before any active gathering.

Over a full 30-minute round (1,800 seconds), 160/sec works out to 288,000 Potions without sending a single extra march. Every additional returned Bone or Idol adds more.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Breach risk">If your territory is breached, you’ll lose some Beast Bones or Runic Idols, which reduces your future passive income. The Cursed Potions you’ve already collected remain safe.</Callout>

## The 30-minute plan

1. **Gather safely from the start.** Turn on your shield and gather Cursed Potions in the Outskirts to lock in rewards with little risk.
2. **Set your defense before entering the core.** Other Doctors may try to sneak-attack you for your Cursed Potions — establish a defense lineup first.
3. **Watch the 10-minute mark.** Combat may intensify around minute 10. Keep relocation items ready and avoid being caught off guard.
4. **High-tier strategy:** stronger Doctors can gather and scout for about **15 minutes**, then target players holding significant Cursed Potions for faster gains.
5. **Coordinate with allies.** Alliance leaders should reach out to nearby alliances to reduce unnecessary conflict and keep gathering efficient.

## Rewards

| Reward | Based on | Core rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event Chests | Your Event Points | Fame Medals, Raven Essence, Diamonds, Herbs |
| Personal Ranking | Your best ranking across all rounds | Alliance Coins, Study Scrolls |
| Alliance Ranking | The alliance’s best ranking across all rounds — **every member receives them** | Curio Chest (UR), UR Hero Omni Shards, Fame Medals |

## Important notes

- **Rankings are settled on your best placement.** A weak round won’t hurt you, so it’s fine to play safe in one round and push in another.
- **If you switch alliances, only the Event Points earned after joining the new alliance count toward that alliance’s total.**

## Quick checklist

- Check the Preview (Tue–Fri) for the earthquake location and Warzones.
- Gather in the Outskirts with a shield for safe points.
- Set a defense lineup before relocating into the Cursed Land.
- Prioritize Runic Idols and bring them home for passive income.
- Have relocation items ready by minute 10.
- Remember: your best round counts.
