Founding Day runs for 5 days, alliance-wide, with individual and alliance-total scoring tracks.
Both tracks matter — alliance-total unlocks a permanent buff for everyone, not just top scorers.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Log in on all five days, even briefly">
  The daily login bonus alone accounts for roughly 15% of an average member's individual score.
  Missing a single day is a bigger loss than most people expect.
</Callout>

## Scoring sources

| Action | Points | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily login | 500 | Once per day, resets at server reset |
| Building upgrade completed | 200 per tier | Counts partial progress toward next tier too |
| Plague zone clear | 150 | Any ring, same value — outer-ring clears are the efficient choice |
| Rally participation | 300 | Must be a listed participant, not just nearby |

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't hoard building upgrades for the event">
  Points come from upgrades *completed* during the window, not started. If a build queue is
  already running when the event starts, it won't count until it finishes inside the window —
  time long upgrades to land after the event opens, not before.
</Callout>

## Alliance-total milestones

BurnFire's alliance-total score unlocks buffs at fixed thresholds — the last one requires
genuinely everyone participating, not just the top 10 scorers.

<Callout variant="lore" title="From last Founding Day">
  "We hit the second milestone with an hour to spare because six people logged in at 11pm just
  to clear one plague zone each. That's the alliance working." — BurnFire founding log, Event #3
</Callout>

Check the officer channel each morning of the event for an updated milestone progress callout —
officers post it manually since there's no in-game alliance-total tracker.
