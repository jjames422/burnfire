## What is waterfall training?

Train lower-tier soldiers during the week, then promote them in stages during troop-training events. The goal is to score from eligible promotions, not to assume time or resource savings.

## Prepare your Training Grounds

Check the highest troop tier each Training Ground can produce. A staggered setup allows successive upgrades — for example, Grounds at Lv.17, 20, 24, and 27 support a T6 → T7 → T8 → T9 pathway. Adapt this to your unlocked tiers and battle needs.

## Build your reserve

Between events, keep your training queues active and produce the lower-tier troops you plan to promote. Save training speedups and enough resources for the entire promotion chain, and keep enough troops ready for combat.

## Promote on the right day

During [Alliance Duel](/guides/alliance-duel) Day 5 (the War Preparation troop-training phase), open a Training Ground, select the lower-tier soldiers, and use the **Promote** option (green arrow). Upgrade one tier at a time where available and repeat through your target tier. Check whether Survival Battle’s troop-training phase overlaps.

## Verify the points

Check the event rules and points after your first small promotion. Different events may score training, promotion, and speedup use differently — never assume every step pays identical points.

## Avoid the common traps

- Don’t upgrade all Grounds to the same level before planning your promotion ladder.
- Don’t spend speedups outside scoring windows without a reason.
- Don’t neglect healing, troop strength, or resource reserves just to chase points.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bottom line">Train consistently, promote deliberately, and compare your total time, resources, and actual event score.</Callout>
