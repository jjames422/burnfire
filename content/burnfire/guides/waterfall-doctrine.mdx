## What waterfall training is

You train lower-tier soldiers during the week, then promote them in stages during troop-training events. The point is to score from eligible promotions on the right day. Don't go in assuming it saves you time or resources — it's a scoring and timing strategy first.

## Set up your Training Grounds

Check the highest troop tier each Training Ground can produce. A staggered setup is what lets you upgrade in successive steps. For example, Grounds at Lv.17, 20, 24 and 27 support a T6 → T7 → T8 → T9 path. Adapt that to whatever tiers you've unlocked and what you need for battle.

## Build your reserve

Between events, keep your training queues running and produce the lower-tier troops you plan to promote later. Hold back training speedups and enough resources to cover the whole promotion chain, and still keep enough troops ready to actually fight.

## Promote on the right day

On Alliance Duel Day 5, the troop-training phase, open a Training Ground, select your lower-tier soldiers and use the Promote option (the green arrow). Go up one tier at a time where you can, and repeat until you reach your target tier. It's also worth checking whether Survival Battle's troop-training phase overlaps that week. The [Alliance Duel guide](/guides/alliance-duel) covers what else scores that day.

## Check the points before going all-in

Do one small promotion first, then look at the event rules and how many points it actually gave you. Different events can score training, promotions and speedup use differently, so never assume every step pays the same.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Common traps">Don't upgrade all your Grounds to the same level before you've planned your promotion ladder. Don't burn speedups outside scoring windows without a good reason. And don't neglect healing, troop strength or resource reserves just to chase points.</Callout>

The honest test is your own numbers: compare the total time and resources you spent against the event score you actually got.
