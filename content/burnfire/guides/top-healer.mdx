## Score from the daily categories

Top Healer is a limited seven-day ranking event. Healing wounded troops is a major point source, while hero fragments, Falcon quests, building, training, research, and other tasks can rotate by day. Higher-tier troops generally award more healing points.

## Prepare before the event

Clear the Infirmary, gather Grain, Timber, and Herbs, stockpile healing speedups, save hero fragments, and keep Falcon quests ready. Once the event begins, complete every daily task group and check the panel for category changes.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keep capacity available">Do not let the Infirmary fill completely. Use healing speedups strategically and spread work across the week instead of rushing on the final day.</Callout>
