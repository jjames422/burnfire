## The question

If no heroes are defending your wall, why are soldiers still dying? Because an empty wall can reduce casualties against a stronger attacker, but it doesn't stop the troops sitting in your Barracks from being killed. Removing defenders is damage control, not immunity.

## Shield first

Go to Sanctuary → Shield and pick a duration that covers the entire danger window. Check the remaining timer before you log off, every time. And recall any gathering marches that are out on the map — a protected Sanctuary doesn't protect troops outside it.

## Know what your wall settings do

Tap Wall → Fortifications and look at the checkboxes next to your assigned squads.

With a squad switched **on**, it defends your Sanctuary. If you're badly outmatched, wounded and dead soldiers pile up fast.

With a squad switched **off**, it no longer garrisons the wall. That can limit your losses, but your Barracks soldiers can still be killed once the wall is breached.

So if you can't win a defense and you have no shield, pulling wall defenders is a reasonable call. Just don't think of it, or describe it to anyone, as zero-loss protection.

## Control your exposure

Check your protected storage, don't open resource packs you don't need yet, and keep an eye on the combat calendar. A shield that runs out while you're offline can leave you open to hit after hit.

## Recover after a hit

Read the battle reports. Check how full your Infirmary is and heal your wounded — a full Infirmary turns the next fight's wounded into permanent losses. Then recheck your Barracks and Rest House counts yourself rather than trusting the battle summary alone.

## Put defenders back when it matters

Some events need wall garrisons, like [Undead Siege](/guides/undead-siege). For those, restore your defenders and follow the event instructions. Leadership will send shield and wall reminders before wars.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Bottom line">Shield beats loss reduction, and loss reduction beats recovery. An empty wall does not mean an empty casualty report.</Callout>
