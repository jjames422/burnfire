**The question:** why do soldiers still die when no heroes defend the wall?

An empty wall may reduce casualties against stronger attackers, but troops in your Barracks can still die. Removing defenders is damage control, **not** immunity.

## Shield first

Open **Sanctuary → Shield**. Choose a duration covering the entire danger window, and check the remaining timer before logging off. Recall exposed gathering marches: a protected Sanctuary does not automatically protect troops outside it.

## Review wall defenses

Tap **Wall → Fortifications** and review the checkboxes beside your assigned squads.

- **On:** those squads defend your Sanctuary. If badly outmatched, wounded and dead soldiers can accumulate quickly.
- **Off:** those squads no longer garrison the wall. This can limit losses, but Barracks soldiers may still be killed after a breach.

If you cannot win a defense and have no shield, consider removing wall defenders — but never describe this as zero-loss protection.

## Control your exposure

Check protected storage, avoid opening unnecessary resource packs, and keep watch on the combat calendar. A shield expiring while you are offline can expose you to repeated attacks.

## Recover after a hit

Read battle reports. Check Infirmary occupancy and heal wounded troops — a full Infirmary can turn later fights into permanent losses. Recheck Barracks and Rest House counts rather than relying on the battle summary alone.

## Restore defenses when needed

For events requiring wall garrisons, such as Undead Siege, restore defenders and follow event instructions. Alliance leaders should send pre-war shield and wall reminders.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Bottom line">Shield &gt; loss reduction &gt; recovery. An empty wall does not mean an empty casualty report.</Callout>
