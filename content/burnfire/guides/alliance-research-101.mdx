Alliance Research lives under a different tab than your personal Research Lab, and it runs on daily donations instead of one-time resource costs — you’ll see a running “Today’s Tech Donation” counter when you open it. It’s split into two full trees, **Development** and **War**, each built as its own ladder of nodes you climb one tier at a time.

## The Development ladder

The ladder starts at **Auto Rally**, then opens into **Fast Build**, **Enhance Help**, and **Gathering Speedup**. Clear those and you unlock **Grain, Timber, and Herb Output**, followed by **Research Speedup** and **Research Upgrade**, then matching **Protection** nodes for all three resources, then **Crafting Speedup** and **Crafting Technique**, capping off at **Construction Cost**.

It’s a long ladder, but every node compounds: faster building, faster gathering, faster research, and cheaper construction all stack on top of each other.

## The War ladder

The War ladder runs in parallel. **Troop Load** branches into **Fast Training**, **Coordinated Training**, and **Garrison Speedup**, which feed into **March Aid** and **Quick Garrison**. From there it splits: toward **Troop March** and the Rally stats (HP, Attack, Defense) on one side, and **Heal Aid** into Garrison stats plus **Barracks Expansion** on the other.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Turn on Auto-Research">There’s an Auto-Research Alliance Tech toggle at the bottom of the screen. Switch it on and your donated points keep climbing the ladder automatically instead of sitting idle waiting on someone to tap the next node by hand.</Callout>
