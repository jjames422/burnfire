## How it's different from your Research Lab

Alliance Research lives under a different tab from your personal Research Lab, and it runs on daily donations rather than one-time resource costs. Open it and you'll see a running "Today's Tech Donation" counter. The tech is split into two full trees, Development and War, and each one is built as its own ladder of nodes you climb one tier at a time.

## The Development ladder

It starts at Auto Rally, then opens into Fast Build, Enhance Help and Gathering Speedup. Clear those and you unlock Grain, Timber and Herb Output, followed by Research Speedup and Research Upgrade, then matching Protection nodes for all three resources, then Crafting Speedup and Crafting Technique. It caps off at Construction Cost.

It's a long ladder, but every node compounds. Faster building, faster gathering, faster research and cheaper construction all stack on top of each other, so the alliance gets quicker at everything the further we push it.

## The War ladder

This one runs in parallel. Troop Load branches into Fast Training, Coordinated Training and Garrison Speedup, which feed into March Aid and Quick Garrison. From there it splits: one side heads toward Troop March and into the Rally stats (HP, Attack, Defense), and the other goes through Heal Aid into the Garrison stats and Barracks Expansion.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The easy win most members miss">There's an **Auto-Research Alliance Tech** toggle at the bottom of the screen. Switch it on and your donated points keep climbing the ladder automatically, instead of sitting idle waiting for someone to tap the next node by hand.</Callout>
