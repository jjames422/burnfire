## What Alliance Research is

Alliance Research is a shared tech tree that the whole alliance funds together. It lives under a **different tab than your personal Research Lab**, and it works differently:

| | Personal Research Lab | Alliance Research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who pays | You | Every member, through donations |
| How you pay | One-time resource cost per level | Daily donations |
| Who benefits | Only your account | The whole alliance at once |
| What you get | Your own stat buffs | Free, permanent stat buffs for everyone |

When you open it, you’ll see a running **“Today’s Tech Donation”** counter tracking what’s been donated that day.

The tree is split into two full ladders: **Development** and **War**. Each is built as a ladder of nodes you climb **one tier at a time** — a tier has to be completed before the next one opens. Both trees are important; they just pay off at different times.

## The Development ladder

Development is about growth: building, gathering, research, and crafting. In order from top to bottom:

1. **Auto Rally**
2. **Fast Build**, **Enhance Help**, **Gathering Speedup**
3. **Grain Output**, **Timber Output**, **Herb Output**
4. **Research Speedup**, **Research Upgrade**
5. **Grain Protection**, **Timber Protection**, **Herb Protection**
6. **Crafting Speedup**, **Crafting Technique**
7. **Construction Cost**

It’s a long ladder, but **every node compounds**. Faster building means you reach higher levels sooner; higher output means more resources to spend; faster research means your personal tech grows faster; protection keeps more of those resources safe from raids; and the final node makes construction cheaper. Faster building, faster gathering, faster research, and cheaper construction all stack on top of each other.

## The War ladder

War is about combat strength: troops, marches, rallies, and garrisons. In order from top to bottom:

1. **Troop Load**
2. **Fast Training**, **Coordinated Training**, **Garrison Speedup**
3. **March Aid**, **Quick Garrison**
4. **Troop March**
5. **Rally HP**, **Rally Attack**, **Rally Defense**
6. **Heal Aid**
7. **Garrison HP**, **Garrison Attack**, **Garrison Defense**
8. **Barracks Expansion**

The ladder splits into two directions after the early nodes: one side builds toward **rally stats** (stronger alliance attacks), the other toward **Heal Aid, garrison stats, and Barracks Expansion** (stronger defense and more troop capacity). Stronger troops, stronger rallies, and stronger garrisons make the difference when soldiers are actually fighting.

## Turn on Auto-Research

At the bottom of the Alliance Research screen is an **Auto-Research Alliance Tech** toggle. This is the easiest win most members miss.

- **With it off,** donated points can sit idle until an officer manually taps the next node.
- **With it on,** your donated points keep climbing the ladder automatically.

## Why timing beats spreading thin

Because every node is shared and compounding, **when** the alliance donates matters as much as **how much**:

- **Donate consistently.** Daily donations keep the ladder moving; skipping days stalls nodes everyone benefits from.
- **Nudge members before key events.** If an event rewards a specific stat — rally strength before a war, for example — getting that node finished first pays off immediately.
- **Donate with purpose.** A full alliance donating on purpose beats a full alliance donating at random, and the difference shows up in every fight.

## Quick checklist

- Find Alliance Research under its own tab, separate from your Research Lab.
- Donate every day — watch the “Today’s Tech Donation” counter.
- Switch on the Auto-Research Alliance Tech toggle.
- Follow leadership calls on which node to push before events.
