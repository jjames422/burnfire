Era of Revival is the first big expansion a server gets once it's been running for a while. Most active players have finished their main research and built up their heroes by then, so the era adds new long-term progression on top. The map stays the same. What changes is how you grow and how you fight: new monsters appear, and against them, your **Resistance (RES)** matters instead of your Might.

The era runs for about **60 days**. It comes before the [Frozen Era](/guides/frozen-era), so if your server doesn't have it yet, this is what's next.

<Callout variant="tip" title="How to tell if your server has it">Look for an Era of Revival icon on your main city screen. That icon opens the Era menu, where everything for the era lives. If it isn't there, your server hasn't reached the era yet.</Callout>

## Prepare before it starts

You don't need to stockpile much, but a few things make a big difference on day one.

**Get Marlena to 10 stars.** Only four heroes matter for the era's upgrades, and each must be at 10 stars before it can be Awakened or Ascended. Marlena is first, and her Awakening week starts on day 1. Claire comes next (her Ascension opens in week 2), then Cynthia and Annie, whose Awakenings come later.

**Save Recruit Tickets.** Red Lady and Daskal join the Tavern when the era begins.

**Save Hero Medals.** They raise the skills of Awakened heroes.

**Consider merging before it starts.** Alliance rewards at the end of the era are based on how your alliance ranks, and the top tiers are far better than the bottom. A strong, active alliance going in matters more than anything you can do mid-era.

Sanctuary Lv.30 helps but isn't required. It's what unlocks T10 troops.

## Day 1 checklist

1. **Build the Lord Statue.** It's under the Military tab in the build menu. Almost everything in the era runs through it.
2. **Build Soldier's Rest.** It's in the same tab. It turns some of your fallen soldiers into resting soldiers that you can send back to your Barracks, which matters once you're fighting era monsters with a full Infirmary.
3. **Open the Era menu** from the Era of Revival icon on your main screen.
4. **Choose your Specialty**, either Handyman or Legion Commander (see below). You get one free reset, so you can switch once if you change your mind.
5. **Kill small Blights quickly.** An event at the start ranks players on Blight kills.
6. **Start the Era Covenant.** Do the daily quests every day, even on the free tier.
7. **Plan Marlena's Awakening** for Alliance Duel Hero Day (more on that below).

## Lord Specialization

At the Lord Statue you pick one of two paths. Your choice doesn't change your rewards, only how you play.

**Handyman** is for growth and support. Pick it if construction, research or resources are what's holding you back. The skills to prioritize are free and instant build or research, the level-30 boosts you can give alliance members, and the level-35 cost reductions.

**Legion Commander** is for combat. Pick it if you rally, reinforce or PvP regularly. Prioritize Return Now, Intensive Training, Boost Morale and Team Raid.

Skills unlock in pairs: the first pair at Specialty level 1, then a new pair every 5 levels up to **level 40**. You can put points into either skill or both, and you don't have to finish one row before moving on. Most skills have cooldowns, so use them as soon as they're ready.

Resets are limited. A **Specialty Reset Book** swaps your path and clears your skills. A **Skill Reset Book** refunds your points but keeps your path. You get one free Specialty Reset Book at the start, and more come from RES achievements, the Era Shop and the paid Covenant tiers. Pick carefully.

### Era Skills

As your Specialty level rises, some bonuses switch on automatically, whichever path you chose. Most of them add Exclusive Weapon Omni Shards to rewards and shops, so leveling Specialty matters even if you don't care about the skills:

| Specialty level | Era Skill |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | One free Direct Relocate a day |
| 15 | Survival Battle daily rewards include Exclusive Weapon Omni Shards (from day 8) |
| 20 | Kingdom War win-streak rewards include Exclusive Weapon Omni Shards |
| 25 | VIP Shop sells Exclusive Weapon Omni Shards (from day 8) |
| 35 | Alliance Shop sells them (from day 8) |
| 40 | Sanctuary Shop sells them (from day 8) |

## Two kinds of EXP

This trips people up. The era uses two separate EXP types, and both are spent in the Specialty Hall at the Lord Statue:

- **Specialty EXP (green book)** raises your Specialty level. It comes from Sandbox monsters, Alliance Duel chests, Kingdom War kill rewards and rallying Guard Blights.
- **RES EXP (purple book)** raises your RES. It comes from an hourly output that grows with your Specialty level, and from beating era monsters.

## Resistance (RES)

Against era monsters, RES is the only stat that counts. If your RES is higher than the monster's, you'll beat it easily. If it's lower, you'll likely lose, however much Might you have. Higher RES also means more damage to era monsters.

**Guard Blights** replace Elite Blights for the whole era. Rally them as often as you can, since they're one of your best sources of Specialty EXP books.

## Hero upgrades

**Awakening (Marlena, Cynthia, Annie).** Each hero gets a 7-day Awakening week, must be at 10 stars, and needs 50 of their own Awaken Shards to Awaken. Each one gets five Awaken Skills built around their faction. Marlena gets stronger with more Warriors on the team, Cynthia with more Rangers, and Annie with more Warlocks, and each one's level-30 skill gives every hero of that faction +8% ATK, HP and DEF.

You don't have to buy the Awaken Pass. Once a hero's week ends, their shards show up in the Daily Offer, which is a cheaper (if slower) way to collect them. If the hero isn't at 10 stars yet, there's no rush.

**Exclusive Weapons (Daskal, Louis, Ulfrid).** Each one arrives on a set day and makes the hero stronger as you upgrade it.

**Claire's Ascension.** From week 2, you can Ascend a 10-star Claire into a stronger UR version that's especially good against monsters. You get the item you need at RES 100 in Era Achievements. Read the rules first: she keeps her level and gear, but drops back to 6 stars, her skills reset, and rebuilding stars costs twice as many shards. Skill Badges are refunded, but not the shards used to get her to 10 stars originally.

## Alliance Duel changes

Two Duel days get new era tasks worth a lot of points:

| Duel day | Era task | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 2 (Construction) | Use 1 Exclusive Weapon Shard | 25,000 per shard |
| Day 4 (Hero) | Use 1 Hero Awaken Shard or Awaken Omni Shard | 25,000 per shard |

That means **Awakening a hero on Hero Day is worth 1,250,000 points** (50 shards × 25,000). Hero Day always falls inside each hero's Awakening week, so waiting for it costs you nothing. Save your Exclusive Weapon Shards for Construction Day the same way. See the [Alliance Duel guide](/guides/alliance-duel) for everything else that scores.

## The Expedition League

This is the era's version of Kingdom War, and it runs for about two months between 16 kingdoms.

- **Sunday to Friday:** kingdoms earn points from events: Alliance Duel wins (30,000) and MVPs (6,000), Demon King Blight, Elixir Scramble wins (50,000), Survival Battle first places, and seizing enemy caravans.
- **Friday:** the kingdom with more points becomes the attacker.
- **Saturday:** the attacker goes for the defender's Royal City.

Round 1 splits the 16 kingdoms into two groups of 8 for three weeks. After a break week, the top four from each group go to the Winners' Bracket and the bottom four to the Losers' Bracket, which decides final places 1–8 and 9–16. **Round 1 decides which bracket you land in, so the early weeks matter most.**

At the end, kingdom rewards go to every player with Sanctuary Lv.15+, and alliance rewards are based on your alliance's **Merit** rank. Within an alliance, the R5 sorts members into five tiers (Leader, Merit, Elite, Veteran and Member) and that choice is final, so agree on the split before settlement.

## Passes and paid extras

- **Era Covenant (Era Pass):** a 60-day pass with a free tier, Epic (about $10) and Mythic (about $20). The free tier is worth doing every day: recruit tickets, Specialty EXP, UR Hero Omni Shards and resources.
- **Era Weekly Pass:** +250 RES and +5% march speed for a week, plus daily Specialty EXP and speedups. It's most useful early, when your RES is low.
- **Greenery city skin:** +100 RES during the era, plus permanent hero bonuses. It's paid.

None of these are required. The free Covenant tier and steady daily play cover the essentials.

## Before the era ends

When the era ends, Specialty and RES EXP books stop dropping, Guard Blights disappear, and your RES goes inactive. **The Specialty Skills you learned stay.** Push your Specialty level as close to 40 as you can before the end. Any Specialty EXP you earn past level 40 isn't wasted: 30% of it comes back by mail as items for the next era.

## How BF2 plays it

Get Marlena to 10 stars before the era lands. Then do the boring things every day: Covenant quests, Guard Blight rallies, and spending your EXP. Awaken on Hero Day and upgrade weapons on Construction Day, because a full Awakening is over a million Duel points. Pick the Specialty that fits what you actually do in game, and use your free reset only if you have to. And show up for the Expedition League's first three weeks, because they decide which bracket we play in for the rest of the era.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The short version">Marlena to 10 stars first. Build the Lord Statue and Soldier's Rest on day 1. Fight era monsters with RES, not Might. Awaken on Hero Day. Reach Specialty level 40 before the era ends.</Callout>
