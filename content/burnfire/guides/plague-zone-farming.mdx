Plague zones reset every 6 hours and most players waste their first clear wandering the outer
ring. These three loops are the ones worth running on a timer.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Run these on a timer, not on availability">
  Set an alarm for the 6-hour reset. Plague zones farmed within the first 20 minutes of reset
  have meaningfully less contention than ones farmed an hour late.
</Callout>

## The three loops

1. **Outer ring scrap loop** — low risk, steady Scrap and Fabric, best for new accounts still
   building up Tier 1/2 structures.
2. **Mid-ring infection nests** — moderate risk, drops Antitoxin (Infirmary upgrade material),
   requires a hero with plague resistance gear.
3. **Inner-ring vault runs** — high risk, best rewards, only worth it in a full squad — solo
   inner-ring runs die more often than they pay off.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't solo the inner ring before Tier 3 gear">
  Inner-ring infection stacks scale faster than outer-ring. A squad without Tier 3 plague
  resistance gear will lose troops to infection ticks, not to enemies.
</Callout>

## Loop comparison

| Loop | Risk | Primary reward | Time per run |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outer ring | Low | Scrap, Fabric | 8 min |
| Mid-ring nests | Medium | Antitoxin | 15 min |
| Inner-ring vault | High | Antitoxin, rare gear | 25 min |
