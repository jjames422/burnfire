## The bubble is a ticket home

During the scheduled Saturday Raid Phase, players are pulled onto an enemy kingdom’s server inside a bubble. It marks them as active raiders; it is not a shield. They can move, scout, and fight while inside it.

The return icon sends a raider directly back to their home kingdom for free. They may even choose their landing location near the alliance flag. This makes a conventional cross-server ambush unreliable: the target can vanish before the trap closes.

## Saturday rules

1. Never teleport into the Hive without an active shield.
2. Never land directly on the alliance flag unless it is an emergency; relocate immediately afterward.
3. Stock relocators in the Alliance Shop before Saturday.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Officer tactic">R4 and R5 leadership can move the alliance flag after returning home, making members harder for opponents to locate and conserving relocators.</Callout>
