Every new shelter starts the same way: not enough scrap, not enough hands, and a plague fog
that doesn't care about your build order. This guide covers the first two days — what to
prioritize, what to ignore, and where new survivors usually waste their first haul of resources.

## Priority order

Build in this order. Skipping ahead to defenses before your Water Reclaimer is the single
most common early mistake.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Start here">
  Rush the **Water Reclaimer** before anything else. Thirst decay is the fastest way to lose
  survivors in the first night, faster than a raid.
</Callout>

1. Water Reclaimer
2. Scrap Storage (upgrade past the starting cap immediately)
3. Perimeter Wall, Tier 1
4. Infirmary
5. Watchtower

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't overbuild storage">
  Storage past Tier 2 is a trap in the first 48 hours — it eats scrap you need for walls, and
  raiders target unguarded stockpiles first. Wait until your perimeter is up.
</Callout>

## Build costs, Tier 1

| Structure | Scrap | Fabric | Build time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water Reclaimer | 120 | 10 | 4h |
| Scrap Storage | 80 | 0 | 2h |
| Perimeter Wall | 60 | 20 | 3h |
| Infirmary | 150 | 40 | 6h |
| Watchtower | 100 | 15 | 5h |

<Callout variant="lore" title="From the founding log">
  "We didn't lose the first shelter to the plague. We lost it to raiders, three hours after

</Callout>

## What to skip early

Don't build a Workshop or start crafting gear in the first 48 hours — the raw materials are
better spent on the list above, and Workshop output is marginal until you've got a stable
water and scrap loop running.

Once your perimeter and Water Reclaimer are stable, move on to the [Clinic tier list](#) for
what to prioritize next.
