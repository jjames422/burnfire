The Dwarven Mine comes around as a limited-time event that runs for about a week. It looks like a luck game, and partly it is. But there's a guarantee built into every floor, and once you know how it works, you can plan exactly how many pickaxes you need. The biggest mistake people make is spending their pickaxes as soon as they get them.

## How it works

Every floor has **12 tiles**. One of them hides the special reward, and the other 11 hold normal rewards. Each dig costs **one Dwarven Pickaxe**. When you dig up a normal reward, that tile is gone, so your odds improve with every miss. Find the special reward and you drop down to the next floor.

There's an auto-mining option if you don't want to tap every tile by hand, and the November 5 patch made it faster.

## Every floor has a guaranteed dig

You never need more than a set number of digs to clear a floor. That cap depends on the last digit of the floor number:

| Floor ends in | Guaranteed on dig |
| --- | --- |
| 1 or 6 | 6th |
| 2 or 7 | 7th |
| 3 or 8 | 9th |
| 4 or 9 | 6th |
| 5 or 0 | 11th |

You'll often hit the special reward earlier than that, but the guarantee means the worst case is known in advance. Floors ending in 5 or 0 are the expensive ones.

## Free pickaxes every day

You can earn **12 free pickaxes a day** from three daily tasks, 4 pickaxes each:

- Train 280 soldiers.
- Complete 1 Falcon Quest.
- Donate to Alliance Tech 6 times.

Over a six-day edition, that's about **72 free pickaxes**. There's also a top-up task that gives 6 more, and pickaxe packs if you spend.

**Your pickaxes stay in your inventory after the event ends.** You don't have to use them all in one edition, and for free-to-play players, that's the whole strategy.

## What each floor pays

On top of the rewards you dig up, you get milestone rewards for reaching certain floors:

| Floor | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Vitrium ×2,000 |
| 10 | Vitrium ×4,000 |
| 20 | Random Vitrium Supply ×8 (1,000–10,000 Vitrium each) |
| 30 | Selectable Gear Supply ×5 |
| 40 | Selectable Gear Supply ×5 |
| 60 | Selectable Gear Supply ×10 |
| 80 | Selectable Gear Supply ×10 |
| 100 | **MR Gear Blueprint ×10** |

Each Selectable Gear Supply lets you pick one of: a UR Gear Blueprint, Tempered Steel ×100, or Vitrium ×3,000.

Floor 100 is the one that matters. MR Gear Blueprints are what you need to promote UR 4★ gear to MR 5★, and there aren't many other ways to get them. Ten of them is a big step toward endgame gear.

You also choose which reward pool to dig for, typically Gear Stones or Herbs. Late-game accounts tend to have more Gear Stones than they can use, while Herbs are always needed for research, so most experienced players go for Herbs. If you're still building gear, Gear Stones may be the better pick for you.

## How many pickaxes you actually need

If every floor from 1 to 99 goes all the way to its guarantee, reaching floor 100 takes **769 pickaxes**, and clearing floor 100 itself takes **780**. That's the true worst case.

You'll almost always find the special reward before the guarantee, so most runs need fewer. Estimates land somewhere between **440 and 670 pickaxes** depending on how lucky you get. Most creators recommend saving **at least 750** before you start a full run, so you're not left stuck at floor 92 with nothing to dig with.

At 72 free pickaxes per edition, a free-to-play player needs roughly ten editions of saving to bank 750. That sounds like a lot, but it's the difference between a handful of Vitrium and ten MR Gear Blueprints.

## How BF2 plays it

Do the three daily tasks every day the event is running. They're things you'd mostly do anyway, and that's where your 12 free pickaxes come from. Then **don't dig**. Let the pickaxes pile up across editions until you have 750 or more, and do the whole climb to floor 100 in one go.

If you're nowhere near 750 and won't be for a long time, it's fine to dig for the early milestones (floors 5, 10 and 20 are cheap and pay Vitrium). Just know that every pickaxe you spend early is one you'll need again later for the MR Blueprints.
