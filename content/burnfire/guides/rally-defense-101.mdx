Most new players lose their first rally defense because they garrison everything in one wave.
Don't. A rally that one-shots your whole garrison is a rally you had no way to win — the fix
is earlier, not harder.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Never full-garrison against an unscouted rally">
  If you can't see the attacking squad's power, assume it's bigger than your garrison cap.
  Scout first, garrison second.
</Callout>

## Before the rally lands

1. Scout the incoming squad the moment you get a warning ping.
2. Pull weaker troops out of garrison — a rally that wipes a full garrison of 5-power troops
   wastes just as many resources as one that wipes a garrison of 50-power troops.
3. Call for reinforcement from officer channel immediately, don't wait to "see how bad it is."

<Callout variant="tip" title="Reinforcement math">
  One reinforcing squad at 80% of your garrison's power roughly doubles your effective defense,
  not adds 80%. Rally defense math is multiplicative on troop formation bonuses, not additive.
</Callout>

## Garrison cap by wall tier

| Wall tier | Garrison cap | Recommended min. defenders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 2,000 | 1 squad |
| Tier 2 | 5,000 | 2 squads |
| Tier 3 | 12,000 | 3 squads, staggered |
| Tier 4 | 25,000 | Full alliance call |

## After the rally

Don't rebuild immediately into the same formation. If a rally beat you once, whoever sent it
knows your composition now — vary your garrison mix before the next window.
