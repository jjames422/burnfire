## Win condition

Two alliances score by holding buildings, fighting, collecting Medkits, and gathering at Camps. The highest score after 30 minutes wins.

## Register and organize

R4/R5 selects up to 20 starters and 10 reserves; the roster locks after registration. Eligibility: top 32 alliances by Might at Monday 00:00, alliance age 7+ days, and Sanctuary Lv15+. Matching uses highest historical Might.

Assign workshop holders, a castle strike team, tent defenders, and scouts. Enter your protected zone during the 5-minute prep (blue = allies; red = enemy).

## 0:00–10:00 — score early

Take both **Alchemy Workshops** (50 alliance pts/sec each). Secure the **Healing Tents** (30/sec each; heal 15 wounded every 10 seconds), the **Observatory** (+10% building-point efficiency), and the **Migration Portal** (relocation cooldown −50%). Keep buildings defended.

## 10:00 — the Castle opens

The **Elixir Castle** earns 80 alliance pts/sec plus a major capture bonus. Coordinate strong squads to seize it. **War Relics** adds 15% allied Hero ATK/DEF/HP; the **Cursed Altar** reduces enemy Hero ATK/DEF/HP by 15%. Each earns 10 alliance pts/sec.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don’t abandon the Workshops">Two Workshops together earn 100 alliance pts/sec — more than the Castle. Don’t abandon them merely to rush the Castle.</Callout>

## 13:00–30:00 — hold and adapt

Camps appear (5 alliance pts/sec plus gathering points). Use spare squads on them while guarding key buildings. Ownership changes scatter Medkits; send scouts to collect them for points.

## Special rules

Special Barracks provide free troops at your highest trainable tier. They cannot die; severe wounds heal free in an unlimited-capacity battlefield tent, though healing takes time. Relocation is free every 2 minutes but recalls marches, and march targets cannot change midway.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Commander’s rule">Assign objectives, reinforce quickly, and watch the scoreboard.</Callout>
