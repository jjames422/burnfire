## How you win

Two alliances score by holding buildings, fighting, collecting Medkits and gathering at Camps. Whoever has the highest score after 30 minutes wins.

## Registering and organizing

An R4 or R5 picks a maximum of 20 starters and 10 reserves, and the roster locks once registration closes. To be eligible, the alliance has to be in the top 32 by Might at Monday 00:00, be at least 7 days old, and have members at Sanctuary Lv15+. Matching is based on highest historical Might.

Before it starts, we assign roles: workshop holders, a castle strike team, tent defenders and scouts. During the 5-minute prep, move into your protected zone. Blue is allies, red is the enemy.

## 0:00–10:00 — score early

Take both Alchemy Workshops first. Each one earns 50 alliance points per second. Then secure the Healing Tents (30 per second each, and they heal 15 wounded every 10 seconds), the Observatory (+10% building-point efficiency), and the Migration Portal (relocation cooldown cut by 50%). Keep all of them defended.

## 10:00 — the Castle opens

The Elixir Castle earns 80 alliance points per second plus a big capture bonus, so coordinate strong squads to take it. War Relics gives allied heroes +15% ATK, DEF and HP, and the Cursed Altar cuts enemy heroes' ATK, DEF and HP by 15%. Each of those earns 10 alliance points per second too.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't abandon the Workshops">Two Workshops together earn 100 alliance points per second, which is more per second than the Castle on its own. Don't leave them just to rush the Castle.</Callout>

## 13:00–30:00 — hold and adapt

Camps start appearing, worth 5 alliance points per second plus gathering points. Send spare squads to them while still guarding the key buildings. Every time a building changes hands it scatters Medkits, so keep scouts moving to pick them up for points.

## Special rules worth knowing

Special Barracks give you free troops at your highest trainable tier. They can't die, and severe wounds heal for free in a battlefield tent with unlimited capacity, though healing takes time. Relocation is free every 2 minutes but recalls your marches. Once a march is sent, you can't change its target midway.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Commander's rule">Assign objectives, reinforce quickly and watch the scoreboard. Points win this event, not kills.</Callout>
